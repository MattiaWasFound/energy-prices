# Group 5: BG, GR, CH, GB, RS, ME (researched 2026-10-04)

## BG - Bulgaria
- VAT: 20% standard rate. No reduced rate for household electricity found. (source: https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/02/10/bulgaria-comprehensive-vat-country-guide-2026/ , secondary; no official NAP page opened)
- Currency: Bulgaria adopted the euro on 2026-01-01 at a fixed 1.95583 BGN/EUR, and the euro has been sole legal tender since 2026-02-01 (https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/en/news/bulgaria-adopts-euro-1-january-2026 , https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/euro/eu-countries-and-euro/bulgaria-and-euro_en). Eurostat's NAC series for BG through 2025-S2 is in BGN (2025-S2 = 0.2650 BGN/kWh incl. taxes, which is about 0.1355 EUR). The baseline must be converted at 1.95583.
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - 2025-05-16: Parliament postponed household market liberalisation indefinitely, so households stay on KEVR/EWRC regulated prices. The "Security of the Electricity System" fund covers the gap to market prices. https://www.bta.bg/en/news/economy/892287-mps-vote-to-postpone-liberalization-of-household-electricity-market-indefinitely
  - 2025-07-01: KEVR raised regulated household prices by +2.56% on average, which is roughly +0.0027 EUR/kWh excl. VAT (estimated). https://sofiaglobe.com/2025/07/01/bulgaria-regulator-approves-2-56-hike-in-electricity-prices-for-household-consumers/
  - 2026-07-01 (regulatory period to 2027-06-30): average +3.05% (Elektrohold +3.14%, EVN +3.28%, Energo-Pro +2.53%). The energy component (Elektrohold area) went to day 0.09297 EUR/kWh (+3.8%) and night 0.04103 EUR/kWh (+6.4%), excl. VAT. Energo-Pro's final day/night tariffs are 0.12927 / 0.07733 EUR/kWh excl. VAT (KEVR decision Ts-13). The average change is about +0.0035 EUR/kWh excl. VAT. https://sofiaglobe.com/2026/07/01/bulgaria-regulator-approves-3-05-hike-in-electricity-prices-for-household-consumers/ , https://www.energo-pro.bg/novini/kevr-utvrdi-novi-ceni-na-elektroenergiyata-ot-1-yuli-2026-g
- Spot exposure: regulated. All households are on KEVR-set day/night tariffs that are reset once a year (July), so a spot-based estimate is misleading.
- Confidence: medium. The regulated tariffs are confirmed. The per-kWh deltas are derived from the percentage changes, and the VAT source is secondary.

## GR - Greece
- VAT: 6% (super-reduced) on household electricity, applied to the whole bill including regulated charges. It has applied since 2019-05-20 (cut from 13%). Note: one search snippet claimed a 23% rate above 200 kWh/month. No primary source supports this, so it is treated as not verified/false. (source: https://energypress.gr/news/ti-symvainei-me-ton-fpa-ton-timologion-reymatos-poy-ehoyn-ekdothei-meta-tin-20i-maioy-2019)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Winter 2024/25: a retail subsidy of about 1.5 c/kWh (green tariffs, households) ran from Dec 2024 to Feb 2025 (approximately; the exact EUR/MWh and end month are not verified on an official page). It has since ended, so it partly affects the 2025-S1 baseline (+~0.015 EUR/kWh for Jan-Feb only). https://www.iea.org/policies/17359-national-measures-against-soaring-energy-prices (seen in search), https://en.parapolitika.gr/economy/31108/greek-electricity-bills-undergo-major-changes-as-green-tariffs-burn-consumers-while-blue-options-offer-savings/ (seen in search)
  - 2026: no household electricity subsidy. A Ministry of Environment and Energy official said on 2026-09-24 that there is "no need for subsidies", though this could be revisited if wholesale goes above 250 EUR/MWh. https://www.in.gr/2026/09/24/economy/energeia-kamia-epidotisi-sto-ilektriko-reyma-ta-senaria-gia-kaysima-kai-aerio/
  - Announced at ΔΕΘ, Sept 2026 (effective 2027-01-01, after the 2026 window): the ΥΚΩ (PSO) charge is halved for the first 1,600 kWh per four months, from 6.90 to 3.45 EUR/MWh (-0.00345 EUR/kWh). The higher ΥΚΩ bands (0.05 / 0.085 EUR/kWh) are unchanged. The government also targets -30% in final prices by 2029 (about 23.8 to 17 c/kWh). https://www.megatv.com/2026/09/12/ti-allazei-stous-logariasmous-reymatos/ , https://www.formedia.gr/2026/09/06/meionontai-50-oi-yko-apo-1is/
- Spot exposure: medium. Variable "green" tariffs are reset monthly from the DAM, and there are also orange dynamic tariffs. Fixed "blue" tariffs have grown to about 32% share (in.gr 2026-09-24).
- Confidence: medium. The VAT and the absence of a 2026 subsidy are clear. The size and duration of the winter 2025 subsidy are not verified officially.

## CH - Switzerland
- VAT: 8.1% standard rate (since 2024-01-01), applied to all tariff components. (source: https://www.steuerinformationen.ch/mehrwertsteuersatz-wird-auf-8-1-angehoben-steuersaetze , secondary; the ESTV page was not opened)
- Changes since 2025-01-01 (ElCom median, profile H4 4,500 kWh/yr, all figures EXCLUDING VAT):
  - 2026-01-01: total 29.0 to 27.7 Rp./kWh. Energy 13.7 to 12.11; network 12.18 to 10.75, plus metering now separate at 74.40 CHF/yr (≈1.65 Rp./kWh). Netzzuschlag (federal grid surcharge) is unchanged at 2.3 Rp./kWh. The Stromreserve (winter reserve) levy went from 0.23 to 0.41 Rp./kWh (+0.18 Rp.). Municipal levies are stable at about 1 Rp./kWh. https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/8nuE_fvwnfqCu8OHNOwKu
  - Published 2026-09-08, effective 2027-01-01: total 26.5 Rp./kWh. Energy 11.35, network 10.94, metering 78.48 CHF/yr, Netzzuschlag 2.3 (unchanged), Stromreserve 0.41 to 0.17 (-0.24 Rp.). https://www.vbs.admin.ch/de/newnsb/1miE201yRzoA
  - Note: the ElCom price site states its figures are excluding VAT ("Vergleich in Rp./kWh (Angaben exkl. MwSt.)", read from the strompreis.elcom.admin.ch app bundle). Some secondary sites (upgrid.ch) wrongly say they include VAT.
- Spot exposure: regulated. Households under 100 MWh/yr are captive in Grundversorgung with annual tariffs set by each DSO. Dynamic tariffs are only emerging.
- Household all-in price: 27.7 Rp./kWh excl. VAT, which is **29.94 Rp./kWh = 0.2994 CHF/kWh incl. 8.1% VAT** (2026 median, H4). For 2027: 26.5, i.e. 28.65 Rp. incl. VAT. As-of: 2026 tariffs (published 2025-09-09). Source: https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/8nuE_fvwnfqCu8OHNOwKu ; data on https://www.strompreis.elcom.admin.ch/ and opendata.swiss (ElCom datasets "Median Strompreis für die Schweiz"/"per Kanton"/"per Stromnetzbetreiber"). Licence: opendata.swiss terms "Freie Nutzung" (free use; seen in the search listing, the dataset page returned 403 so this is not opened directly).
- Confidence: high. The only caveats are that the VAT source is secondary and the licence was read from a search listing.

## GB - United Kingdom (Great Britain)
- VAT: **0% on domestic electricity in GB from 2026-10-01 to 2027-03-31** (temporary zero-rating, cut from 5%; announced 2026-07-21, funded for FY 2026/27 only). Northern Ireland is excluded and gets comparable funding instead. The rate reverts to 5% from 2027-04-01 unless extended at the Autumn Budget. Gas stays at 5%. (source: https://www.gov.uk/government/news/breathing-space-on-your-energy-bill ; https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/your-energy-supply/your-energy-bill/energy-price-cap-unit-rates-and-standing-charges ; NI exclusion per https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-gb/legal/news/prime-minister-s-office-announces-vat-removal-on-domestic-electricity-bills-from-1-october-2026)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - 2026-04-01 (Budget 2025 policy-cost move): 75% of the Renewables Obligation moved from bills to the Exchequer for three years (≈ -£70/yr typical, all on electricity). The ECO bill charge ended (≈ -£63/yr typical dual fuel, split across electricity and gas). The government's total figure is -£134 typical DD (avg £150). Warm Home Discount costs moved between standing charge and unit rate; network costs rose by about £65. The cap electricity unit rate (DD, incl. 5% VAT) went from 27.69 to 24.67 p/kWh; the standing charge from 54.75 to 57.21 p/day. Net policy effect on the electricity unit rate is roughly -3 p/kWh excl. VAT (my estimate from £/yr at ~2,700 kWh; Ofgem gives no per-kWh split). https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/transparency-document/changes-eco-and-ro-energy-price-cap-april-2026 , https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/press-release/energy-price-cap-will-fall-7-april , https://www.energy-uk.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Energy-UK-Price-cap-Q2-26.pdf
  - 2026-10-01: VAT 5% to 0% on domestic electricity (above). This is about -£45/yr typical.
- Spot exposure: low. About 60% of customers are on the quarterly Ofgem cap and the rest are mostly on fixed deals. Half-hourly/agile tariffs are a small niche.
- Household all-in price: Ofgem cap Q4 2026 (1 Oct to 31 Dec 2026), Direct Debit, GB average: **unit rate 26.32 p/kWh + standing charge 54.83 p/day, VAT 0% included**. The previous quarter (Jul-Sep 2026, incl. 5% VAT) was 26.11 p and 57.19 p/day. Effective all-in at the Ofgem TDCV of 2,700 kWh/yr: 26.32 + 54.83×365/2700 ≈ **33.73 p/kWh (0.3373 GBP/kWh)**. As-of: published 2026-08-26, valid 2026-10-01 to 2026-12-31. Source: https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/news/changes-energy-price-cap-between-1-october-and-31-december-2026 . Licence: Ofgem website content, generally Open Government Licence v3 (not verified on the page). Eurostat has not covered GB since 2020.
- Confidence: high for the cap and VAT. Low for the per-kWh size of the April 2026 policy-cost removal (estimated).

## RS - Serbia
- VAT: 20% on electricity, plus a 7.5% excise on electricity. Renewable-energy fee 0.801 RSD/kWh and energy-efficiency fee 0.015 RSD/kWh are added before excise and VAT. (source: https://www.blokvolt.rs/en/podaci/tarife-eps/ , secondary; the Ministry of Finance page was not opened)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - 2025-10-01 (AERS-approved, EPS guaranteed supply): average +6.6%, due to higher transmission and distribution access charges. Green zone day 9.1092 to 9.6136 RSD/kWh (+0.504) and night 2.2773 to 2.4034. Blue day 13.6638 to 14.4203. Red day 27.3276 to 28.8407 (all excl. excise/VAT). The blue/red zone threshold was lowered from 1,600 to 1,200 kWh/month (up to +1.9% extra for affected users). https://www.eps.rs/lat/vesti/Stranice/nova-cena-ee-gs.aspx , https://www.danas.rs/vesti/ekonomija/poskupljenje-struje-cenovnik-racuni-tarife/
  - 2026: no change so far. The IMF arrangement commits to an increase of at least inflation +1pp (~4.5%) by Oct 2026. As of 2026-09-12 EPS had not filed with AERS, and with 30 days' notice required, no hike is expected before the 2026-10-25 elections. A post-election increase is likely but not yet decided. https://insajder.net/teme/hoce-li-struja-poskupeti-pre-izbora-korekcija-cene-sa-mmf-om-dogovorena-za-oktobar-eps-jos-bez-zahteva
- Spot exposure: regulated. Almost all households are on EPS guaranteed supply at AERS-approved zone/tariff prices.
- Household all-in price: Eurostat nrg_pc_204 (DC band, all taxes, NAC) **2025-S2 = 13.9473 RSD/kWh** (2025-S1 12.9118). It only partly reflects the Oct 2025 +6.6%. Fully applied, a reasonable 2026 estimate is about 14.4-14.9 RSD/kWh (my estimate, not official). As-of: 2025-S2 (Eurostat update 2026-09-24). Source: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/nrg_pc_204?format=JSON&nrg_cons=KWH2500-4999&unit=KWH&tax=I_TAX&currency=NAC&geo=RS . Licence: Eurostat reuse policy (CC BY 4.0). Eurostat covers RS. For comparison, the green-zone day rate is 13.45 RSD/kWh all-in (blokvolt).
- Confidence: medium. The VAT/excise source is secondary, and the timing of the expected post-election hike is unknown.

## ME - Montenegro
- VAT: 21% standard rate (since 2018-01-01), applied to electricity. A new VAT law (2026) keeps 21%. (source: https://www.ekapija.com/news/1989622/u-crnoj-gori-povecana-stopa-pdv-a-na-21-nove-cene-elektricne , https://investitor.me/2026/06/27/novi-zakon-o-pdv-u-ceka-ulazak-u-eu-stopa-ostaje-21-ali-pravila-za-biznis-postaju-mnogo-stroza/)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - 2026-01-01: EPCG's 6.4% discount on network services, which applied throughout 2025, was not renewed in its 2026 discount decision (≈ +3% on the average bill). REGAGEN's 2026 provisional distribution prices do not repeat the 2025 network-price corrections (≈ +2.2% on the average bill). The active-energy price is unchanged. Combined, this is roughly +0.004 EUR/kWh excl. VAT (my estimate: ~5% of ~0.0825). https://www.cdm.me/ekonomija/gradani-se-zale-na-vece-januarske-racune-za-struju-epcg-veca-potrosnja-i-ukidanje-popusta-podigli-iznose/ , https://rtcg.me/vijesti/ekonomija/613604/epcg-projektuje-da-ce-do-2028-cijena-struje-godisnje-rasti-sest-odsto.html
  - Forward-looking: EPCG projects about +6%/yr on active energy for 2026-2028 (≈43 to 54 EUR/MWh by 2028), but no increase for 2026 was found. REGAGEN signals lower transmission fees for 2027-2029 (from CGES surplus). https://pvportal.me/2026/08/regagen-struja-za-domacinstva-u-crnoj-gori-medju-najjeftinijima-u-evropi/
- Spot exposure: regulated. EPCG is the de facto sole household supplier at prices that have been essentially flat for about 15 years.
- Household all-in price: **0.0998 EUR/kWh incl. all taxes and VAT** (REGAGEN, 2025 average household price; matches Eurostat nrg_pc_204 DC 2025-S2 = 0.0998 EUR, 2025-S1 0.0973). For 2026, about 0.103-0.105 EUR/kWh would follow from the network-discount removal (estimate). As-of: 2025 (published Aug 2026). Source: https://pvportal.me/2026/08/regagen-struja-za-domacinstva-u-crnoj-gori-medju-najjeftinijima-u-evropi/ ; https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/nrg_pc_204?format=JSON&nrg_cons=KWH2500-4999&unit=KWH&tax=I_TAX&currency=NAC&geo=ME . Licence: Eurostat CC BY 4.0; no licence stated for REGAGEN. Eurostat covers ME.
- Confidence: medium. The 2026 network-fee impact is given as bill percentages, not per-kWh.
