# Group 3: FR, ES, PT, IT, IE, AT (researched 2026-10-04)

## FR - France
- VAT: 20% on the whole bill (energy, abonnement, accise, CTA) since 2025-08-01; before that the abonnement and CTA were at 5.5% and consumption at 20%. (source: https://selectra.info/energie/guides/comprendre/taxes/tva ; https://pro.engie.fr/electricite-gaz/conseils-et-actus/augmentation-TVA-electricite)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Accise sur l'électricité (ex-TICFE/CSPE), households <=36 kVA: 21.00 EUR/MWh (shield rate, to 2025-01-31) -> 33.70 (2025-02-01) -> 29.98 (2025-08-01, cut to offset the VAT change on the abonnement) -> 30.85 (2026-02-01) -> 30.62 (2026-08-01). Calendar-2025 average about 31.1 EUR/MWh, so today is about -0.5 EUR/MWh against the 2025 average (+0.6 against 2025-S2). (https://www.fournisseurs-electricite.com/contrat-electricite-gaz/taxes/accise-electricite ; https://www.hellowatt.fr/blog/revalorisation-accise-electricite-fevrier-2026/)
  - VAT on the abonnement and CTA went from 5.5% to 20% on 2025-08-01. This hits the fixed part only, no per-kWh effect. The government offset it with the accise cut. (selectra link above)
  - TRVE (regulated tariff): -15% TTC on 2025-02-01 (Base 0.2516 -> 0.2016 EUR/kWh TTC); stable on 2025-08-01; -0.83% TTC on 2026-02-01 (CTA 21.93% -> 15%, TURPE unchanged); +2.5% TTC on 2026-08-01 (Base kWh +3.1%, now 0.2001 EUR/kWh TTC for 3-6 kVA). (https://www.hellowatt.fr/blog/nouveaux-trve-fevrier-2025/ ; https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053423540 ; https://www.cre.fr/actualites/nos-lettres-dinformation/la-cre-propose-de-maintenir-les-tarifs-reglementes-de-vente-de-lelectricite-ttc-stables-en-moyenne-au-1er-fevrier-2026-pour-les-consommateurs-souscrivant-une-puissance-inferieure-a-36-kva.html ; https://www.hellowatt.fr/blog/hausse-trve-aout-2026/ ; https://www.cre.fr/fileadmin/Documents/Deliberations/2026/260715_2026-147_TRVE.pdf)
  - TURPE 7 HTA-BT: about +3.04% on 2026-08-01, which CRE puts at about +1% TTC on the TRVE. My estimate is +0.001 to +0.0015 EUR/kWh on the energy part. (https://entreprises-collectivites.engie.fr/actualites/turpe-comprendre-tarif-utilisation-reseaux-publics-electricite/ ; https://www.cre.fr/fileadmin/Documents/Deliberations/2026/260204_2026-33_Modif_TURPE_7_HTB_HTA_BT.pdf)
  - CTA (a % of the network fixed part): 21.93% -> 15% on 2026-02-01. Fixed part only.
  - Context, not verified in detail here: ARENH ended 2025-12-31, so from 2026 the TRVE energy block is priced from market forwards.
- Spot exposure: regulated - most households are on the TRVE (EDF "tarif bleu") or on offers indexed to it. The TRVE energy price is set from forward-market averages, not day-ahead, so a spot-based estimate is misleading.
- Confidence: medium. The accise path is well sourced, but only from secondary sites. The 2025-08 TURPE movement is not verified, and the per-kWh TURPE effect is my own estimate.

## ES - Spain
- VAT: 21% (IVA general). It was cut temporarily to 10% from 2026-03-22 for contracts <=10 kW (RDL 7/2026). Because the April energy CPI was below the 15% threshold, the cut ended early and 21% returned on 2026-06-01. RDL 18/2026 lets the cut be reinstated if prices rise. The Canary Islands use IGIC, not IVA (general knowledge, not re-verified). (source: https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/marzo/23/medidas-materia-tributacion.html ; https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-noticias/2026/junio/30/medidas-tributarias-real-decreto-ley-182026.html)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - IVA was 21% for all of 2025 (the 10% regime ended 2024-12-31). Temporary 10% from 2026-03-22 to 2026-05-31, then 21% again from 2026-06-01 (links above).
  - Impuesto Especial sobre la Electricidad (an ad-valorem % on energy and power terms, not per kWh): 5.11269632% in 2025. Cut to 0.5% from 2026-03-22 to 2026-05-31, then back to 5.11269632% from 2026-06-01. (https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuestos-especiales-medioambientales/novedades-impuestos-especiales-medioambientales/2026/marzo/25/medidas-extraordinarias-impuesto-especial-sobre-medio.html)
  - Cargos (system charges), 2.0TD energy term, from 2026-01-01: P1 0.058305 -> 0.064292, P2 0.011661 -> 0.012858, P3 0.002915 -> 0.003215 EUR/kWh, about +10% (Orden TED/1524/2025 vs TED/1487/2024). At a typical P1/P2/P3 split of about 30/25/45% that is about +0.0022 EUR/kWh. (https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2025-26705 ; https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2024-27289)
  - Peajes (network tolls) for 2.0TD: about -1.3% from 2026-01-01 (CNMC resolution of 2025-12-18). That is about -0.0004 EUR/kWh, my estimate. (https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2025-26348 ; https://www.ocu.org/vivienda-y-energia/gas-luz/noticias/subida-costes-regulados-2026)
  - PVPC formula: the forward-market weight rose from 40% (2025) to 55% (2026), so 45% is day-ahead/intraday (RD 446/2023). (https://okdiario.com/economia/tu-tarifa-regulada-luz-cambia-2026-que-significa-nuevo-pvpc-que-sera-menos-volatil-16071759)
- Spot exposure: medium - PVPC (roughly a third of households, a figure I did not verify) is hourly-priced, but in 2026 only 45% of it is day-ahead/intraday and 55% is forwards. The free market is mostly fixed-price.
- Modelling note: the IEE is a percentage (x1.0511) applied before VAT, not a per-kWh add-on.
- Confidence: high on taxes. Medium on the net per-kWh figure, which uses an assumed period split.

## PT - Portugal
- VAT: standard 23%. Since 2025-01-01 a reduced 6% applies to the first 200 kWh/month (300 kWh for large families) for contracted power <=6.9 kVA, and consumption above that is taxed at 23%. A DC-band household (~3500 kWh/yr, ~290 kWh/month) pays an effective rate of about 11-13% on energy. (source: https://www.erse.pt/media/tcsfm4n2/ersexplica_iva-fatura_2025.pdf ; https://www.jornaldenegocios.pt/economia/detalhe/e-oficial-iva-da-luz-vai-descer-a-partir-de-janeiro-de-2025)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - VAT reduced band widened from 100 to 200 kWh/month (300 for large families), effective 2025-01-01. This is already inside the 2025 Eurostat data. (links above)
  - Tarifas de Acesso às Redes, BTN (households): +3.5% on 2026-01-01. ERSE's chart puts the BTN average at about 84 -> 87 EUR/MWh, so about +0.003 EUR/kWh. I read that value off a garbled chart, so confidence is low. (https://www.erse.pt/media/lmrp4h2q/perguntasrespostas_tarifasele_2026.pdf)
  - Regulated transitional tariff (CUR): +1.0% on 2026-01-01; an extraordinary energy-component revision of +2.7% from 2026-10-01 (Middle-East gas shock). (same ERSE link ; https://observador.pt/2026/09/15/crise-energetica-ja-contaminou-eletricidade-precos-no-mercado-regulado-vao-subir-27-em-outubro/)
  - Social tariff discount kept at 33.8%; no change to IEC or other levies found.
- Spot exposure: low - more than 87% of BTN consumption is in the free market (ERSE, Sep 2025), mostly on fixed-price offers. Indexed/dynamic OMIE offers exist but are a minority. The CUR regulated tariff (~817k clients) is set quarterly.
- Confidence: medium. The VAT rule is official. The per-kWh network change is approximate, and the blended effective VAT depends on the consumption profile.

## IT - Italy
- VAT: 10% for domestic use (whole bill); 22% for non-domestic. Accisa is 0.0227 EUR/kWh, with exemptions on the first 150 kWh/month for resident customers <=3 kW. (source: https://www.servizioelettriconazionale.it/it-IT/tariffe/costo-fornitura/tabella-imposte-energia-elettrica)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Oneri generali, ASOS, domestic customers (residents and non-residents): 0.028657 EUR/kWh (held through 2025 and into H1 2026) -> 0.031515 EUR/kWh from 2026-07-01 (+0.002858, about +10%, ARERA delib. 227/2026/R/com). ARIM is 0.001638 EUR/kWh (Q4 2026), and I did not verify its 2025 level. I did not directly verify the 2025 domestic ASOS either: the 0.028657 figure comes from early-2026 supplier sheets, and ARERA's Q4-2025 report said it was keeping the ASOS/ARIM rates at their existing levels. (https://www.servizioelettriconazionale.it/content/dam/sen/componenti-asos-arim.pdf ; https://www.mysolution.it/fisco/informazioni/news/2026/07/03/dal-1-luglio-energia-elettrica-piu-cara-sale-la-tariffa-per-gli-inventivi-alle-rinovabili/ ; https://www.arera.it/area-operatori/prezzi-e-tariffe/oneri-generali-di-sistema-e-ulteriori-componenti ; https://www.arera.it/fileadmin/allegati/docs/25/429-2025-R-com_Relazione_tecnica.pdf)
  - Decreto Bollette 2026: a one-off EUR 115 contribution for 2026 to bonus sociale holders (ISEE <=9,796, or <=20,000 for families with 4+ children), plus a voluntary supplier discount of up to EUR 60 in 2026-27 for residents with ISEE <=25,000. These are lump sums with no per-kWh effect. (https://www.irenlucegas.it/consigli-utili/bonus-e-agevolazioni/decreto-bollette-2026 ; https://www.fiscal-focus.it/quotidiano/altre-tematiche/economia-societa/decreto-bollette-2026-cosa-prevede-tutte-le-misure,3,183279)
  - Tutela: maggior tutela for non-vulnerable households ended in July 2024 (they moved to STG). About 3M vulnerable customers remain in tutela, and their price rose +4.6% in Q3 2026. (https://teleborsa.ansa.it/notiziario/economia/elettricita-arera-prezzo-riferimento-utenti-vulnerabili-sale-del-46/)
  - VAT and accisa unchanged.
- Spot exposure: medium - variable offers indexed to PUN/PUN Index GME are common, but they are mostly billed on monthly averages rather than hourly. Many households are on fixed-price offers, and vulnerable customers are on a quarterly ARERA price.
- Confidence: medium (2025 domestic ASOS level inferred, not read from an ARERA table).

## IE - Ireland
- VAT: 9% on electricity (a temporary reduction from 13.5%), extended by Budget 2026 to 2030-12-31. (source: https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-finance/press-releases/t%C3%A1naiste-announces-budget-2026-tax-changes-to-come-into-effect-on-1-january-2026/ ; https://www.bdo.ie/en-gb/insights/2025/budget-2026/budget-2026-vat)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - PSO levy, domestic (a fixed monthly charge, ex VAT): EUR 3.23/month (2024/25) -> 1.46/month (2025/26, reduced rate from 2025-12-01) -> 0.51/month (2026/27, from 2026-10-01). That is about -EUR 32.6/yr against 2024/25. (https://www.cru.ie/about-us/news/cru-approves-202627-electricity-network-charges-and-reduced-pso-levy/ ; https://cruie-live-96ca64acab2247eca8a850a7e54b-5b34f62.divio-media.com/documents/2025-26_PSO_Final_Decision_PaperCRU2025108.pdf)
  - Network charges, typical domestic: +~EUR 29/yr from 2025-10-01 and +~EUR 41.25/yr from 2026-10-01 (PR6). (https://www.cru.ie/about-us/news/cru-approves-annual-electricity-network-charges/ ; CRU 2026/27 link above)
  - Net effect of the PSO and network changes: about +EUR 37.6/yr. Spread over a 4,200 kWh typical household that is about +0.009 EUR/kWh. Both items are mostly standing charges, so the per-kWh equivalent is approximate.
  - Electricity Credit: Budget 2025 paid EUR 250 in two EUR 125 instalments (Dec 2024 / early 2025). Budget 2026 has no universal credit. If Eurostat netted the 2025 credits into the household price, the 2025 add-on is understated by up to ~EUR 125 per half-year, which is very large per kWh. I did not check Eurostat's treatment, so check it before relying on the 2025-S1 baseline. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Irish_budget ; https://selectra.ie/energy/news/budget-2026-ireland)
- Spot exposure: low - most households are on fixed unit rates or smart time-of-use rates. Dynamic tariffs only became mandatory for large suppliers by 2026-06-01, and uptake is new and small. (https://www.bonkers.ie/guides/gas-electricity/dynamic-electricity-tariffs-here-s-everything-you-need-to-know/)
- Confidence: medium (the changes are mostly fixed charges converted to per-kWh, and the treatment of the credit in Eurostat is unknown).

## AT - Austria
- VAT: 20% (standard). No reduced rate on household electricity. (source: https://stromliste.at/strompreis/zusammensetzung/steuern - secondary; I did not open an official VAT page)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Elektrizitätsabgabe, households: 0.1 c/kWh (2024 crisis rate) -> 1.5 c/kWh from 2025-01-01 -> 0.1 c/kWh (EU minimum) from 2026-01-01, a temporary measure for 2026 only. Businesses pay 0.82 c/kWh. Change against 2025: -0.014 EUR/kWh. (https://www.parlament.gv.at/aktuelles/news/Nationalrat-beschliesst-Senkung-der-Elektrizitaetsabgabe ; https://services.bundeskanzleramt.gv.at/newsletter/bka-medien-newsletter/innenpolitik/bka-medieninformation-16-12-2025.html)
  - Stromkostenbremse (subsidy on the first 2,900 kWh) ended 2024-12-31. The Erneuerbaren-Förderpauschale (EUR 19.02/yr at NE7, unchanged 2025-2027) and the Förderbeitrag were reinstated from 2025-01-01, and network fees rose sharply on 2025-01-01. All of this is already in the 2025 Eurostat baseline. (https://ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20012807&FassungVom=2026-02-24&Artikel=&Paragraf=1&Anlage=&Uebergangsrecht= ; stromliste link)
  - Erneuerbaren-Förderbeitrag: -14% in 2026 per the BKA (about -EUR 10/yr at 3,500 kWh, i.e. about -0.0003 EUR/kWh). Secondary sources say -22%. (BKA link above)
  - Netzentgelte 2026: the average increase was capped at +1.1% (about +0.0001 EUR/kWh, my estimate). (BKA link above)
  - Sozialtarif (ElWG, BGBl I 91/2025): from 2026-04-01, 6 c/kWh net energy price on the first 2,900 kWh/yr for about 290k eligible low-income households. It is targeted, so it does not apply to the average. (https://stromliste.at/strompreis/tarife/sozialtarif ; https://kea.gv.at/sozialtarif-strom/)
- Spot exposure: medium - smart-meter coverage is high and hourly dynamic tariffs (e.g. aWATTar) exist. Most households are on fixed or monthly-floater (ÖSPI) tariffs. I did not find a quantified share.
- Confidence: high for the Elektrizitätsabgabe. Medium for the smaller items (sources disagree on the Förderbeitrag %).
