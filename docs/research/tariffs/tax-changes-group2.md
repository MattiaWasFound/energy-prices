# Group 2: LT, PL, DE, NL, BE, LU (researched 2026-10-04)

All per-kWh amounts are excl. VAT unless marked "incl. VAT".

## LT - Lithuania
- VAT: 21% standard rate on household electricity. VERT's public-supply price is 17.877 ct/kWh excl. VAT and 21.631 ct/kWh incl. VAT, a factor of exactly 1.21 (source: https://vert.lt/elektra/Puslapiai/archyvas/Vidutines-visuomenines-elektros-energijos-kainos-nuo-2026-sausio-1-iki-birzelio-30.aspx). The reduced 9%/12% rates that appear in searches are for heat, not electricity. VMI rate page: https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/pvm-tarifai (it did not state the electricity rate explicitly).
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - ESO distribution tariff, 2026-01-01: single-zone plans +0.01002 EUR/kWh incl. VAT (about +0.00828 excl. VAT). Two-zone and four-zone plans: +0.0064 to +0.0161 EUR/kWh incl. VAT. https://ignitis.lt/naujienos/kaip-keiciasi-elektros-kaina-2026 and https://www.eso.lt/namams/elektra/tarifu-planai-kainos-atsiskaitymas/tarifu-planai-ir-kainos-2026-metais/4801
  - SPPD (distribution additional component), 2026-01-01: 0.00108 -> 0.00027 EUR/kWh incl. VAT, so -0.00067 excl. VAT (same Ignitis URL).
  - VIAP (PSO levy), 2026-01-01: -0.00047 -> -0.00053 EUR/kWh incl. VAT; -0.044 ct/kWh excl. VAT for 2026. The levy stays slightly negative. https://www.vert.lt/Docs/D%C4%96L%20VIE%C5%A0UOSIUS%20INTERESUS%20ATITINKAN%C4%8CI%C5%B2%20PASLAUG%C5%B2%20L%C4%96%C5%A0%C5%B2%20IR%20KAINOS%202026%20METAMS%20NUSTATYMO.pdf
  - Net change in the add-on: about +0.0076 EUR/kWh excl. VAT from 2026-01-01.
  - Public-supply tariffs (Ignitis) rose +2 ct/kWh incl. VAT from 2026-07-01, to about 24 ct (standard) and 20 ct (vulnerable). Public supply only covers vulnerable households and those using under 1000 kWh/yr. This is the energy part of a regulated price, not a change to the add-on. https://sc.bns.lt/view/item/patvirtinti-2026-m-antrojo-pusmecio-visuomeninio-elektros-tiekimo-tarifai-530957
- Spot exposure: medium. Most households have been with independent suppliers since the 2023 market opening, on fixed or exchange-linked plans; Ignitis offers market-linked "Smart" plans. Small users and vulnerable users are on regulated public supply. I did not find a share of households on spot-linked plans.
- Confidence: medium. The component deltas come from supplier and VERT pages. The spot-exposure share is unverified.

## PL - Poland
- VAT: 23% standard rate in 2026. The temporary 5% rate ended in 2024. A proposal to cut it back to 5% exists but has not been legislated, as far as I found. (source: https://www.vatcalc.com/poland/poland-vat-country-guide/ (secondary); https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Electricity_price_statistics)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Price freeze ended 2025-12-31. The statutory cap was 500 PLN/MWh net. From 2026-01-01 households pay URE-approved G tariffs, which average 495.16 PLN/MWh net: -0.0048 PLN/kWh against the cap. URE puts the typical G11 bill (about 1.8 MWh/yr) at about +3%. https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/urzad/informacje-ogolne/aktualnosci/13002,Prezes-Urzedu-Regulacji-Energetyki-zatwierdzil-taryfy-na-sprzedaz-i-dystrybucje-.html
  - Distribution tariffs, 2026-01-01: +9.36% on average. Not converted to per-kWh, because it varies by DSO (same URE URL).
  - Capacity fee (oplata mocowa), 2026-01-01, net per month: 16.01 -> 24.05 PLN for >2800 kWh/yr, and 11.14 -> 17.18 PLN for 1200-2800 kWh/yr. At 3500 kWh/yr that is about +0.0276 PLN/kWh. https://www.gramwzielone.pl/trendy/20339842/rachunki-za-energie-wzrosna-ure-podal-stawke-oplaty-mocowej-na-2026
  - OZE (renewables) levy, 2026-01-01: 3.50 -> 7.30 PLN/MWh, i.e. +0.0038 PLN/kWh. The cogeneration fee stays at 3.00 PLN/MWh. https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/urzad/informacje-ogolne/aktualnosci/13002,Prezes-Urzedu-Regulacji-Energetyki-zatwierdzil-taryfy-na-sprzedaz-i-dystrybucje-.html
  - Net change in quantified items: about +0.0266 PLN/kWh (energy -0.0048, capacity +0.0276, OZE +0.0038). This excludes the distribution increase of +9.36%.
- Spot exposure: regulated. Almost all households are on URE-approved G tariffs, and in 2025 they were on the 500 PLN/MWh freeze. A spot-plus-add-on estimate is misleading here. Dynamic tariffs exist but are marginal.
- Confidence: medium. The levy and freeze facts come from URE. The per-kWh distribution change is not quantified, and the VAT source is secondary.

## DE - Germany
- VAT: 19% on the whole bill, including all levies (source: https://www.verivox.de/strom/themen/strompreiszusammensetzung/ and https://www.bdew.de/presse/pressemappen/strompreis/ (secondary; statutory basis is UStG §12)).
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Grid fees, 2026-01-01: a 6.5 bn EUR federal (KTF) subsidy to the TSOs' transmission grid fees cuts the average household grid fee by about -1.95 ct/kWh net (to about 9.04 ct/kWh at 4000 kWh, -17.8%). The subsidy continues at 5.525 bn EUR/yr for 2027-2029. https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/niedrigere-netzentgelte-2382396 and https://www.enet.eu/aktuelles/bundeszuschuss-entfaltet-deutliche-wirkung-auf-stromnetzentgelte
  - Levies, 2026-01-01: KWKG 0.277 -> 0.446 ct/kWh; Offshore-Netzumlage 0.816 -> 0.941 ct/kWh; §19 StromNEV surcharge 1.558 -> 1.559 ct/kWh. Together that is +0.295 ct/kWh. https://www.wattline.de/energiewissen/strom-umlagen/ and https://www.netztransparenz.de/de-de/Erneuerbare-Energien-und-Umlagen/Sonstige-Umlagen/Aufschlag-f%C3%BCr-besondere-Netznutzung-19-StromNEV-Umlage/%C3%9Cbersicht-Aufschlag-f%C3%BCr-besondere-Netznutzung/Aufschlag-f%C3%BCr-besondere-Netznutzung-2026
  - Stromsteuer: NOT cut for households. It stays at 2.05 ct/kWh. The cut to the EU minimum from 2026-01-01 applies only to manufacturing, agriculture and forestry. The coalition committee declined a cut for households. https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/niedrigere-netzentgelte-2382396 and https://www.zdfheute.de/politik/deutschland/stromsteuer-koalition-ausschuss-keine-einigung-100.html
  - Gasspeicherumlage abolished 2026-01-01. This affects gas only, not electricity. https://www.svo.de/magazin/energie-news-2026-das-aendert-sich-bei-mobilitaet-heizen-strom-und-verbraucherschutz
  - Net change in the add-on: about -1.66 ct/kWh net (-0.01655 EUR/kWh) from 2026-01-01.
- Spot exposure: low. Since 2025-01-01 every supplier must offer a dynamic tariff (§41a EnWG), but only about 5.5% of households had smart meters at end-2025, so take-up is small. Most households are on fixed or Grundversorgung contracts. https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2025/20251202_Stromtarife.html
- Confidence: high on the levies and the Stromsteuer. Medium on the exact grid-fee delta, which is an average and varies by DSO.

## NL - Netherlands
- VAT: 21% on the whole bill, including energy tax (source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/energie-thuis/opbouw-energierekening).
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Energiebelasting (electricity, 0-10,000 kWh brackets), 2026-01-01: 0.10154 -> 0.09161 EUR/kWh excl. VAT, i.e. -0.00993 EUR/kWh. https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/overige_belastingen/belastingen_op_milieugrondslag/energiebelasting/energiebelasting
  - Belastingvermindering (fixed rebate per connection), 2026-01-01: 524.95 -> 519.80 EUR/yr excl. VAT, i.e. -5.15 EUR/yr (628.96 EUR incl. VAT). This is fixed, not per kWh; it adds roughly +0.0015 EUR/kWh at 3500 kWh (same URL).
  - DSO network tariffs are fixed per-connection charges in NL, so they are not per kWh. I did not verify the 2026 changes to them.
- Spot exposure: medium. Dynamic contracts numbered 936k at end-2025 (about 7% of contracts) and about 8% in early 2026, growing fast ahead of the 2027 end of net metering (saldering). Most households are on fixed or variable contracts. https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/energiemonitor-acm-tarieven-licht-omhoog-aantal-huishoudens-met-dynamisch-contract-blijft-licht-stijgen and https://energienerds.nl/index.php/2026/01/21/dynamische-energiecontracten-passeren-900-000/
- Confidence: high.

## BE - Belgium
- VAT: 6% on household electricity, permanent since 2023-04-01 and unchanged in 2026 (source: https://news.belgium.be/nl/btw-verlaging-op-elektriciteit-en-verhoging-van-btw-vrijstellingsdrempel and https://financien.belgium.be/sites/default/files/downloads/faq-elektriciteit.pdf).
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Federal special excise on electricity (residential): 50.33 -> 48.76 EUR/MWh in 2026, i.e. -0.00157 EUR/kWh. Further steps are 45.58 (2027), 42.40 (2028) and 40.28 (2029). The energy contribution (energiebijdrage) is abolished, and gas excise rises in parallel. The programme law was delayed (vote postponed in March 2026), so the cut took effect on 2026-08-01, not 2026-01-01. https://www.inforgazelec.be/nl/reforme-accise-cotisation-energie/ and https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/07/28/accijnsverhoging-aardgas-stookolie-vanaf-1-augustus-energiefactu/ and https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/03/25/uitstel-stemming-programmawet-gevolgen/
  - Regional grid tariffs, 2026-01-01:
    - Flanders: electricity distribution about -4% (about -20 EUR/yr incl. VAT at 3500 kWh, roughly -0.0054 EUR/kWh); the capacity tariff structure is unchanged.
    - Brussels (Sibelga): about +6% (+25 EUR/yr, to about 493 EUR/yr).
    - Wallonia (ORES etc.): new tariff structure (off-peak band 11:00-17:00), costs +4 to +8%.
    - Not summed into the national figure, because the regions move in opposite directions. https://www.engie.be/nl/blog/groener-leven/energie-aanpassingen-2026-budget/ and https://selectra.be/nl/energie/nieuws/nettarieven-2026
  - Net quantified national change: -0.00157 EUR/kWh, from the excise only.
- Spot exposure: medium. True dynamic contracts are rare (about 0.4% of the market; suppliers with >200k customers must offer one). Variable contracts, which are common, are indexed monthly to wholesale prices, so households pass through spot with a lag. https://www.dayaheadmarket.eu/belgium (secondary)
- Confidence: medium. The excise path and the 2026-08-01 effective date are from news and sector sources, not the Moniteur. The regional grid deltas are approximate.

## LU - Luxembourg
- VAT: 8% reduced rate on electricity in 2026. The temporary 7% rate applied only in 2023. (source: https://www.avalara.com/fr/fr/vatlive/country-guides/europe/luxembourg/luxembourgian-vat-rates.html (secondary; consistent with gov.lu examples))
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - The grid-fee subsidy is new from 2026, not a phase-out. In 2024-2025 the state cut prices through a negative compensation-mechanism (MdC) contribution, -11.55 ct/kWh in 2024 and -3.76 ct/kWh in 2025. https://www.ilr.lu/wp-content/uploads/publication/ilr-elc-pub-2025-08-20-Rapport-MDC-2024.pdf and https://www.chd.lu/en/node/2497
  - From 2026-01-01, for at least 3 years, the state pays about one third of network costs (150 m EUR in 2026): network component 13.3 -> about 9.5 ct/kWh for the STATEC typical household (3901 kWh). The state also takes over the MdC contribution, which goes from -3.8 to about -0.1 ct/kWh. The net change in the add-on against 2025 is about -0.1 ct/kWh. The government's figure for the typical household is 28.6 ct/kWh in 2025 and 25.8 ct/kWh in 2026, the fall coming mainly from a lower energy price. https://gouvernement.lu/dam-assets/images-documents/actualites/2025/12/02-delles-aides-electricite/20251202-confrence-de-presse-nouvelles-aides-tatiques-pour-allger-la-facture-dlectricit.pdf and https://meco.gouvernement.lu/fr/domaines-activites/energie/marches-de-lenergie/subvention-electricite.html
  - Temporary subsidy, 2026-08-01 to 2026-12-31: 4 ct/kWh incl. VAT (about -0.037 EUR/kWh excl. VAT) for residential customers using under 25,000 kWh/yr. It is applied automatically and expires 2026-12-31. https://gouvernement.lu/fr/actualites/toutes_actualites/communiques/2026/07-juillet/14-delles-subventions-prix-gaz-electricite.html
  - Sum as of today: about -0.038 EUR/kWh. Only about -0.001 of that is permanent; the rest lapses after 2026-12-31.
- Spot exposure: low. Households are mostly on fixed supplier tariffs (Enovos, Sudstroum, etc.). Dynamic offers are rare.
- Confidence: medium. The component values are read from a government chart, and the VAT source is secondary.
