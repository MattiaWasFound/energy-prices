# Group 4: CZ, SK, HU, SI, HR, RO (researched 2026-10-04)

## CZ - Czechia
- VAT: 21% standard rate; electricity has no reduced rate in 2026. The only temporary cut (0% VAT, Nov-Dec 2021) is long gone. (source: https://financnisprava.gov.cz/assets/cs/prilohy/fs-financni-sprava-cr/EtR_Dan_z_pridane_hodnoty_2026.pdf ; ERU price pages below)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - From 2026-01-01 the state budget pays POZE (renewables support). The POZE component on bills went from 495 CZK/MWh to 0 CZK/MWh (-0.495 CZK/kWh, excl. VAT). Government decision of 2025-12-16, implemented in ERU's amended price decision. https://eru.gov.cz/eru-vydal-zmenovy-cenovy-vymer-kterym-upravuje-regulovane-ceny-na-rok-2026
  - Net effect on the whole regulated component for low voltage (households): 2,772 CZK/MWh (2025) -> 2,352 CZK/MWh (2026), i.e. -420 CZK/MWh = -0.42 CZK/kWh excl. VAT (-15.1%). This already includes about +75 CZK/MWh from distribution increases (about +7% on average). https://eru.gov.cz/eru-vydal-zmenovy-cenovy-vymer-kterym-upravuje-regulovane-ceny-na-rok-2026 ; https://eru.gov.cz/eru-vydal-cenove-vymery-kterymi-stanovi-regulovane-ceny-elektriny-plynu-na-rok-2026-0
  - The regulated part has fixed per-month charges as well as per-kWh charges, and distribution varies by DSO area and tariff. The -0.42 CZK/kWh figure is ERU's average per MWh.
- Spot exposure: low - fully liberalised market where most households are on fixed-price products; spot-indexed products exist but are a minority.
- Confidence: high for the POZE change (regulator source); medium for the exact net per-kWh figure (an average across tariffs).

## SK - Slovakia
- VAT: 19% reduced rate on electricity supply from 2025-01-01 (Act 278/2024). Before that it was 20% standard; the standard rate is now 23%. The 23% standard rate does not apply to electricity. (source: https://www.financnasprava.sk/sk/podnikatelia/dane/dan-z-pridanej-hodnoty/sadzby-dane ; https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/slovakia-increases-vat-rate)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - VAT 20% -> 19% on electricity, 2025-01-01. https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/slovakia-increases-vat-rate
  - Regulated commodity price (URSO), 2026-01-01:
    - 2025: 61 EUR/MWh for all households.
    - 2026 base price: 103.94 EUR/MWh.
    - Energopomoc households (household "bonita" income <= 1,930 EUR/month; reported as up to ~90% of households): suppliers must cut the price by 31.24 EUR/MWh, giving 72.70 EUR/MWh.
    - So aided households pay +11.70 EUR/MWh (+0.0117 EUR/kWh) and non-aided households +42.94 EUR/MWh (+0.0429 EUR/kWh). https://www.urso.gov.sk/ceny-energii-na-rok-2026/ ; https://www.energie-portal.sk/Dokument/ceny-energii-elektriny-plynu-tepla-energopomoc-2026-112022.aspx
  - Network charges, 2026-01-01:
    - TPS (system operation): 15.90 -> 14.071 EUR/MWh.
    - TSS (system services): 12.4207 -> 10.9796 EUR/MWh.
    - Total distribution + transmission: 48.742 -> 46.8653 EUR/MWh, i.e. -1.88 EUR/MWh.
    - National Nuclear Fund levy unchanged at 3.27 EUR/MWh.
    - URSO's average final household price rose +2.78% (190.66 -> 195.95 EUR/MWh incl. VAT). https://www.urso.gov.sk/ceny-energii-na-rok-2026/
  - Net per-kWh change for aided households: +11.70 - 1.88 = about +0.0098 EUR/kWh. For non-aided households it is about +0.041 EUR/kWh.
- Spot exposure: regulated - households are supplied at URSO-regulated prices (2026 includes a state-subsidised price); a spot-based estimate is misleading.
- Confidence: medium (regulator figures are solid, but which households receive energopomoc, and so the mix, is uncertain).

## HU - Hungary
- VAT: 27% standard rate, applied to household electricity; there is no reduced rate. (source: https://www.mvmnext.hu/lakossagirezsi/reszletes-informaciok-az-aramszamlarol - prices are stated as "brutto (27% afaval egyutt)")
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Rezsicsökkentés regulated A1 prices unchanged:
    - Up to 2,523 kWh/yr (about 210 kWh/month): about 36 HUF/kWh gross (30.988-43.409 HUF/kWh by DSO area, all-in incl. network and VAT).
    - Above the limit: 70.104 HUF/kWh gross.
    - https://www.mvmnext.hu/lakossagirezsi/reszletes-informaciok-az-aramszamlarol
  - Household network-use fees were frozen for 2026 under Government Decree 351/2025 (emergency powers, published 2025-11-12); non-household fees fell about 12%. No per-kWh change for households. https://wattler.eu/2026/01/villamos-rhd-2026/ (secondary)
  - 2026-09-01: MVM introduced a new optional "D tariff". Consumption up to the subsidy limit stays at the A1 fixed price; consumption above the limit follows day-ahead exchange prices. No change for default customers. https://www.portfolio.hu/gazdasag/20260902/uj-aramtarifat-jelentett-be-az-mvm-dontesi-helyzetbe-kerultek-a-rezsicsokkentett-haztartasok-859986
  - Note: the Eurostat DC band (2500-4999 kWh) straddles the 2,523 kWh limit, so it mixes the 36 and 70 HUF/kWh prices.
- Spot exposure: regulated - universal-service prices are fixed by government; the optional D tariff (from Sept 2026) only exposes consumption above the limit.
- Confidence: high that there were no changes; medium on the freeze detail (secondary source, the decree itself was not opened).

## SI - Slovenia
- VAT: 22% standard rate. The temporary 9.5% rate on energy applied 2022-09-01 to 2023-05-31 and has ended. (source: https://www.energetika-portal.si/podrocja/energetika/ukrepi-za-omilitev-draginje-na-podrocju-energetike/elektricna-energija/)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Regulated retail price cap ended:
    - It was in force until 2025-02-28 (winter 2024/25 caps excl. VAT: VT 0.084, MT 0.070, ET 0.077 EUR/kWh).
    - From 2025-03-01 supplier price lists follow the market.
    - Under a spot+add-on model, this makes the Eurostat 2025-S1 baseline understate the add-on for Jan-Feb.
    - https://www.energetika-portal.si/podrocja/energetika/ukrepi-za-omilitev-draginje-na-podrocju-energetike/elektricna-energija/
  - OVE+SPTE (renewables/CHP) contribution for households. It is charged per kW of billing power per month, not per kWh.
    - Fully waived until 2025-06-30.
    - 50% from 2025-07-01 to 2026-02-28.
    - Full rate of 0.77562 EUR/kW/month from 2026-03-01.
    - About +20 EUR/yr for a small household and +50 EUR/yr for a large one; roughly +0.005 to +0.013 EUR/kWh.
    - From 2027 (ZSROVE-1) it becomes per-kWh, about 0.00845 EUR/kWh (not yet fixed).
    - https://www.energetika-portal.si/podrocja/energetika/ukrepi-za-omilitev-draginje-na-podrocju-energetike/elektricna-energija/ ; https://www.zurnal24.si/pod-streho/varcna-hisa/konec-znizanj-od-marca-na-poloznicah-polna-cena-455605
  - Network charges (omrežnina): the new 5-block, two-season, 15-min tariff system has applied since 2024-10-01.
    - The Agency's 2026 tariffs apply from 2026-03-01 (Jan-Feb kept the old tariffs).
    - Energy tariffs in blocks 1-2 rose 11% and 9%; block-1 power tariff rose 3.4225 -> 3.82301 EUR/kW.
    - Household network cost is about +25 to +55 EUR/yr (about +36%), roughly +0.01 EUR/kWh.
    - Block-1 household power tariff is phased in: 40% (Nov-Dec 2025), 60% (2026), 80% (2027).
    - https://www.zurnal24.si/pod-streho/varcna-hisa/pozor-spet-novosti-pri-obracunavanju-porabe-elektrike-451714 ; https://www.delo.si/gospodarstvo/novice/kaksne-omrezninske-spremembe-predlaga-agencija-za-energijo (secondary; Agency documents not opened)
  - Net per-kWh vs the 2025-S1 baseline (excl. energy): about +0.02 EUR/kWh (OVE about +0.01, network about +0.01). This is a rough conversion from per-kW/yearly figures.
- Spot exposure: low - market prices since March 2025, but households overwhelmingly on fixed supplier price lists; dynamic offers are rare.
- Confidence: medium on the facts, low on the per-kWh figure (the charges are power-based).

## HR - Croatia
- VAT: 13% reduced rate on electricity supply (incl. related charges), under VAT Act Art. 38(3); unchanged in 2026. (source: https://porezna-uprava.gov.hr/hr/porez-na-doda-nu-vrijednost/4579)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Regulated energy price (Government Uredba), 2025-11-01, for consumption up to 3,000 kWh per half-year:
    - Single tariff: 0.079412 -> 0.091324 EUR/kWh (+0.011912).
    - VT: 0.084512 -> 0.097189 EUR/kWh.
    - NT: 0.041468 -> 0.047688 EUR/kWh.
    - Surcharge above 3,000 kWh per half-year cut from +50% to +35%.
    - https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_09_121_1723.html ; https://www.racunalo.com/hep-cijena-struje-veca-za-15-posto-od-1-studenog-2025-godine/
  - The same prices were extended for 2026-04-01 to 2026-09-30 and again for 2026-10-01 to 2027-03-31 (Uredba NN 101/2026, 11th package). https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2026_09_101_1209.html
  - Network fees (HERA), 2026-01-01: HEP ODS +15%, HOPS +10%. A typical household (about 3,105 kWh/yr) pays 5.34 -> 6.06 c/kWh (+0.0072 EUR/kWh). https://serbia-energy.eu/croatia-approves-new-electricity-network-fees-average-household-costs-to-rise-by-13-5-in-2026/ (secondary; HERA decision not opened)
  - Renewables (OIE) fee: 0.013239 EUR/kWh, no change found. https://www.hep.hr/elektra/kucanstvo/tarifne-stavke-cijene/1547
  - Net per-kWh change: about +0.0191 EUR/kWh (excl. VAT).
- Spot exposure: regulated - household energy price set by government decree; spot estimate misleading.
- Confidence: high on energy prices (official gazette); medium on network (secondary source).

## RO - Romania
- VAT: 21% from 2025-08-01 (was 19%; Law 141/2025). Electricity is at the standard rate; the new single reduced rate of 11% does not cover it. (source: https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/AsistentaContribuabili_r/Cotele_de_TVA_09.2025.pdf)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - The cap-and-compensation scheme ended 2025-06-30, after running since 2021.
    - Capped final prices were 0.68 RON/kWh, 0.80 RON/kWh and 1.30 RON/kWh by consumption band (incl. VAT; roughly <=100, 100-255 and >255 kWh/month).
    - Post-cap offers are about 1.3-1.5 RON/kWh incl. VAT; bills rose about 61% on average.
    - For a household on about 300 kWh/month: about 0.70 -> 1.12 RON/kWh excl. VAT, about +0.42 RON/kWh.
    - The Eurostat 2025-S1 figure for RO reflects capped prices, so the add-on baseline is badly understated.
    - https://www.businessforum.ro/energy/20250630/energy-price-caps-in-romania-to-end-after-more-than-4-years-1972 ; https://www.energynomics.ro/en/fel-romania-explains-electricity-bills-with-the-end-of-cap-and-compensation-schemes/ ; https://www.romania-insider.com/uncapped-electricity-prices-inflation-romania-august-2025
  - VAT 19% -> 21%, 2025-08-01. https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/AsistentaContribuabili_r/Cotele_de_TVA_09.2025.pdf
  - Vulnerable households get 50 RON/month vouchers from Jul 2025 to Mar 2026. This is income support and does not change the tariff.
  - Network tariffs (ANRE), 2026-01-01:
    - Transelectrica +about 10% (33.03 -> 36.45 RON/MWh extraction component).
    - LV distribution: PPC/REER +8% (317.4 RON/MWh), DEER +1.25%, Delgaz +9.8%, Oltenia -8%.
    - Roughly +0.02 RON/kWh on average (figures are ANRE proposals as reported; final orders not opened).
    - Green certificates about 0.0725 RON/kWh and CHP contribution 0.0136 RON/kWh (2026 supplier sheet).
    - https://www.digi24.ro/stiri/economie/energie/anre-a-propus-majorarea-tarifelor-la-energie-de-la-1-ianuarie-2026-oltenia-e-singura-regiune-unde-tariful-de-distributie-scade-3550883 ; https://www.ppcenergy.ro/wp-content/uploads/tarife-reglementate-energie-electrica-01-01-2026-.pdf
  - Net per-kWh change: about +0.44 RON/kWh excl. VAT (cap removal +0.42, network +0.02). This is very approximate. Most of it is energy cost that a spot model captures via wholesale; the true add-on shift depends on how Eurostat recorded the capped prices.
- Spot exposure: low - fully liberalised since July 2025, but households are on supplier fixed or universal-service offers; dynamic contracts are rare.
- Confidence: medium on the facts; low on the per-kWh number.
