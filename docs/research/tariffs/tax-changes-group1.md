# Group 1: DK, NO, SE, FI, EE, LV (researched 2026-10-04)

## DK - Denmark
- VAT: 25% standard rate, no reduced rate for electricity. (Standard rate. No change found; the VAT URL itself was not opened.)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Elafgift (household general rate) cut from ~72.0 øre/kWh in 2025 to 0.8 øre/kWh, the EU minimum (excl. VAT). Temporary for 2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31. Without the cut the indexed 2026 rate would have been 72.7 øre. Net change about -71.2 øre/kWh. Sources: https://skat.dk/erhverv/afgifter-paa-varer-og-ydelser-punktafgifter/nyhedsbrev-afgifter/midlertidig-nedsaettelse-af-elafgiften-i-2026-og-2027 , https://svmn.dk/tal-og-metode/satser/satser-og-beloebsgraenser-i-lovgivningen/elafgiftsloven ; 2025 = 90 øre incl. VAT per https://nrgi.dk/privat/stroem/bliv-klogere-paa-elprisen/elafgiften-falder-fra-1-januar-2025/
  - Energinet consumer tariffs (system 7.2 + transmission net 4.3) fall from 13.5 to 11.5 øre/kWh from 2026-01-01, a change of -2.0 øre. They are capped at ≤12.5 øre for 4 years. Source: https://via.ritzau.dk/pressemeddelelse/14552884/elforbrugernes-tarif-bliver-naeste-ar-115-ore-og-falder-dermed-med-15-procent?publisherId=10304728&lang=da
  - The local DSO (netselskab) tariffs were not researched. They vary by DSO and are time-of-use.
- Spot exposure: high. Most Danish retail contracts are variable and pass through the hourly or quarter-hourly spot price.
- Confidence: high for the elafgift cut. Medium for the exact 2025 base (72.0 øre is from a utility and news, not the ministry page).

## NO - Norway
- VAT: 25% standard. Household electricity in Nordland, Troms and Finnmark is VAT-exempt (merverdiavgiftsloven § 6-6). (source: https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/rettskilder/type/handboker/merverdiavgiftshandboken/gjeldende/M-6/M-6-6/)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Elavgift (forbruksavgift) is now one flat rate. In 2025 it was 9.79 øre/kWh (Jan-Mar), 16.93 (Apr-Sep) and 12.53 (Oct-Dec); the simple monthly average is ~14.05. From 2026-01-01 it is 7.13 øre/kWh all year (excl. VAT). That is a net change of about -6.9 øre/kWh against the 2025 average, or about -2.7 against Jan 2025. The reduced rate of 0.60 øre applies in tiltakssonen (Finnmark and Nord-Troms). Sources: https://www.glitrenett.no/kunde/nettleie-og-priser/Forbruksavgift , https://lovdata.no/dokument/STV/forskrift/2025-12-18-2760/KAPITTEL_1#%C2%A72
  - Norgespris started 2025-10-01 and runs to 2026-12-31 (re-enrolment needed for 2027). It is an optional state fixed energy price of 40 øre/kWh **excl. VAT**, so 50 øre incl. VAT; (some sources quote 40 øre as incl. VAT; it is excl. VAT). It covers up to 5,000 kWh/month for homes and 1,000 for cabins. It replaces the spot energy price for enrolled meters. Uptake on 2026-08-31 was 1.58M households, 59.9% of eligible meters: 64-77% in NO1/NO2/NO5, ~47% in NO3, and a small minority in NO4. Sources: https://www.energiaktuelt.no/39-milliarder-kroner-spart-med-norgespris-i-andre-kvartal-2026.6753455-575597.html , https://www.fortum.com/no/strom/blogg/sa-mange-har-valgt-norgespris
  - Strømstøtte, for households not on Norgespris, continues through 2026. It covers 90% of the hourly spot price above the threshold. The threshold rose from 75 to 77 øre/kWh excl. VAT on 2026-01-01. It is credited on the nettleie invoice and applies up to 5,000 kWh/month. Source: https://www.elinett.no/news/stroemstoetteordningen-viderefoeres-i-hele-2026-terskelverdien-endres-fra-75-til-77-oere
  - The Enova levy (1 øre/kWh) was not verified and is assumed unchanged.
- Spot exposure: low. About 60% of household meters, and the large majority in the populous NO1/NO2/NO5, now pay the fixed Norgespris of 40 øre excl. VAT instead of spot. For the rest, spot above 77 øre is 90% subsidised. A spot-based estimate is misleading except in NO4.
- Confidence: medium. The elavgift and Norgespris figures are well sourced, but the per-kWh "change" depends on which 2025 baseline is used, and Norgespris breaks the spot+add-on model.

## SE - Sweden
- VAT: 25% standard. No change found. (The VAT URL was not opened; the 25% rate is implied by the Skatteverket and energimarknadsbyrån figures, where 36.0 excl. VAT = 45.0 incl.)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - Energiskatt on electricity cut from 43.9 to 36.0 öre/kWh (excl. VAT) on 2026-01-01, a change of -7.9 öre. No end date is stated. Source: https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/skatterochavdrag/punktskatter/nyheterinompunktskatter/2025/nyheterinompunktskatter/sanktskattpael1januari2026.5.1522bf3f19aea8075ba96f.html
  - The northern municipalities' deduction of 9.6 öre/kWh is unchanged, so the effective rate there is 26.4 öre. Same source.
- Spot exposure: high. Hourly and monthly variable contracts are dominant.
- Confidence: high.

## FI - Finland
- VAT: 25.5% general rate, in force since 2024-09-01, so no change in the window. (The VAT figure is confirmed indirectly by Caruna's "incl. 25.5% VAT": https://caruna.fi/en/news/strategic-stockpile-fee-included-electricity-tax-will-increase-1-april-2026)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - The strategic stockpile (security-of-supply) fee inside the class I electricity tax rose from 0.013 to 0.085 c/kWh excl. VAT on 2026-04-01, a change of +0.072 c/kWh. The total class I tax went from 2.253 to 2.325 c/kWh excl. VAT. The energy content tax of 2.24 c/kWh is unchanged. Sources: https://www.vero.fi/en/businesses-and-corporations/taxes-and-charges/excise-taxation/sahkovero/Tax-rates-on-electricity-and-certain-fuels/ , https://caruna.fi/en/news/strategic-stockpile-fee-included-electricity-tax-will-increase-1-april-2026
  - No other national change was found. DSO tariff changes were not researched.
- Spot exposure: medium. Exchange-price (pörssisähkö) contracts are common but fixed-price contracts remain widespread. The share was not verified.
- Confidence: high for the tax. Low for the spot-exposure split.

## EE - Estonia
- VAT: 24% from 2025-07-01 (22% before), now permanent. Electricity has no reduced rate. (source: https://emta.ee/en/business-client/taxes-and-payment/value-added-tax/vat-rates-and-supply-exempt-tax/standard-vat-rate ; https://www.ey.com/en_ee/insights/tax/significant-tax-changes-in-estonia-in-2025-2026)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - VAT rose from 22% to 24% on 2025-07-01 (sources above).
  - Electricity excise rose from 1.45 to 2.10 EUR/MWh on 2025-05-01, then to 3.07 EUR/MWh on 2026-05-01. Total change +0.162 c/kWh. Sources: https://www.sakumaja.ee/elektrienergia-aktsiis-touseb-alates-1-maist-45-protsenti/ , https://www.err.ee/1609677845/mai-alguses-tousevad-mitmed-aktsiisid
  - A new security of supply charge (varustuskindluse tasu) of 0.758 c/kWh excl. VAT started 2026-01-01 on the network invoice. It is collected for Elering. Source: https://elektrilevi.ee/en/uudised/ulevaade-2026-aasta-vorguteenuse-arvest-lisandub-varustuskindluse-tasu-taastuvenergia-tasu-ei-muutu
  - A new balancing capacity fee (tasakaalustamisvõimsuse tasu) of 3.73 EUR/MWh (0.373 c/kWh) started 2026-01-01 on the supplier invoice. It was not charged separately in 2025. Source: https://www.elering.ee/artikkel/tasakaalustamisvoimsuse-tasuks-kujuneb-373-eurot-megavatt-tunni-kohta
  - The renewable energy charge is 0.84 c/kWh, unchanged through 2026. It fell from 1.05 c/kWh on 2025-01-01; that cut is already in the 2025 baseline and is excluded from the sum. Sources: https://elektrilevi.ee/en/uudised/ulevaade-2026-aasta-vorguteenuse-arvest-lisandub-varustuskindluse-tasu-taastuvenergia-tasu-ei-muutu , https://viruev.ee/taastuvenergia-tasu-alates-1-jaanuarist-2025/
  - The Elektrilevi network charge rose about 2.6% on 2025-08-01, through fixed parts only (kWh prices unchanged). The average network price rises about 3.4% to ~4.66 c/kWh from June 2026. Source: https://elektrilevi.ee/en/vorguleping/vorgutasu-muudatus
  - The universal service, a regulated price option, ended 2026-04-30. Source (secondary): https://rahakalkulaator.ee/blogi/elektri-toetus-ja-arve-2026/
- Spot exposure: high. Exchange-price packages are dominant, and the universal-service regulated price option ended 2026-04-30.
- Confidence: medium. The fee amounts come from the TSO and DSO, but the excise steps come from secondary sources (news and a company site) and Riigi Teataja was not opened. The network-tariff per-kWh effect is left out of the sum.

## LV - Latvia
- VAT: 21% standard on household electricity. No reduced rate and no change found. (source: https://eng.lsm.lv/article/economy/economy/05.12.2025-some-power-bills-to-rise-in-2026-in-latvia.a625252/ ; secondary for the rate: https://gramatvedisriga.lv/en/blog/vat-rates-2026)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - The OIK (mandatory procurement + capacity component) stays at 0 EUR/MWh. It has been zero since 2023 and continues into 2026, so there is no change. Source: https://www.em.gov.lv/lv/jaunums/indriksone-turpmak-visiem-elektroenergijas-lietotajiem-oik-rekina-ailites-bus-nulle
  - The distribution tariff itself is unchanged in 2026. However, state compensation of the 2023 tariff increase ended on 2026-01-01. Fixed monthly capacity charges for ≤25 A "Basic" household connections rise by EUR 0.20-1.26/month (1-phase) and EUR 1.92-3.50/month (3-phase), excl. VAT. These are fixed charges, not per kWh. Sources: https://eng.lsm.lv/article/economy/economy/05.12.2025-some-power-bills-to-rise-in-2026-in-latvia.a625252/ , https://www.sprk.gov.lv/events/kas-2026-gada-mainisies-regulejamas-nozares
  - Transmission tariff for households is unchanged in 2026; it is embedded in distribution tariffs (SPRK, same source).
  - Balancing capacity maintenance costs, previously covered by AST, have been charged since 2025-07-01. Balance-responsible parties pay 3.00 EUR/MWh on consumption (H2 2025), and suppliers likely pass this on in retail prices. That is about +0.3 c/kWh, but the pass-through and the 2026 level are not verified; SPRK expects lower balancing costs in 2026. Source: https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/latvia/energy-and-natural-resources/new-requirement-for-balancing-service-recipients-to-cover-the-costs-of-maintaining-balancing-capacity-in-latvia
- Spot exposure: medium. Both exchange-linked and fixed contracts are offered and widely used; the share was not verified.
- Confidence: medium/low. The VAT and OIK are solid, but the per-kWh balancing pass-through is uncertain and the fixed-charge rise is not per-kWh.
