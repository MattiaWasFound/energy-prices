# Group 6: MK, BA, AL, XK, UA, MD (researched 2026-10-04)

Eurostat coverage check (nrg_pc_204, band KWH2500-4999, queried via the Eurostat dissemination API on 2026-10-04,
https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/nrg_pc_204): MK, BA, AL, XK, MD have data through
2025-S2; UA returned no data for 2024-S1 onward. The I_TAX/X_VAT ratio in that data matches the VAT rates below
(MK 1.18, BA 1.17, AL 1.20, XK 1.08, MD 1.00), except MK 2024-S1 (1.10).

## MK - North Macedonia
- VAT: 18% on household electricity. The preferential rate was cut 18% -> 5% (mid-2021), raised to 10% (2023-01-01), and returned to 18% on 2023-07-01. (sources: https://24.mk/details/od-1-juli-poskapuva-strujata (2023-06-27, "ДДВто од 10 на 18 отсто" from 1 July); https://www.slobodnaevropa.mk/a/%D0%BC%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%BE-%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%BA%D0%B0%D1%87%D1%83%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%9A%D0%B5-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D1%81%D1%82%D1%80%D1%83%D1%98%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B0-%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D0%B3%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%93%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5-%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%BE-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%BC%D0%B0%D0%BB%D1%83%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%9A%D0%B5-%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D0%BC%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5-%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BC%D0%BF%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B8/32197213.html; Eurostat I_TAX/X_VAT = 1.18 for 2024-S2 to 2025-S2.) Caveat: the Finance Ministry VAT page (https://finance.gov.mk/mk-MK/oblasti/danok-na-dodadena-vrednost) still lists household electricity under the 10% preferential rate. That page looks stale given the Eurostat ratio.
- Changes since 2025-01-01 (regulated universal-supply block tariffs, EVN Home, controlled by ERC/RKE):
  - 2025-01-01: block increases of +0.68% (B1), +0.9% (B2), +1.93% (B3), +2.24% (B4) and +0.9% (cheap tariff). https://frontline.mk/2025/01/01/vo-novata-godina-so-minimalno-zgolemena-cena-na-stru-ata-i-ponatamu-dnevna-evtina-tarifa-od-13-do-15-chasot/
  - 2026-01-01: +2.45% (B1), +2.87% (B2), +3.32% (B3), +3.84% (B4) and +2.5% (low tariff). 85% of households see about +2.3%. The ESM wholesale price went from 62 to 65 EUR/MWh. New HT1 is 4.7074 MKD/kWh and LT 2.1893 MKD/kWh. https://glasotnaevropa.mk/2026/01/od-ovaa-godina-graganite-ce-placaat-poskapa-struja-od-245-do-384-otsto/ ; https://www.evn.mk/Invoices.aspx?lang=en-gb
  - Net effect: the national average household price incl. VAT went from 7.271 MKD/kWh (H1 2025) to 7.427 (H1 2026), about +0.13 MKD/kWh excl. VAT (approximate).
- Spot exposure: regulated - households are almost all on the regulated universal-supply block tariff.
- Household all-in price: 7.427 MKD/kWh incl. VAT (average for households, H1 2026), from the State Statistical Office (DZS), reported in https://mk.bloombergadria.com/ekonomija/makedonija/114098/dzs-rast-na-prosecnata-cena-na-strujata-i-na-gasot-vo-makedonija-vo-prvoto-polugodie-od-2026/news. Eurostat 2025-S2 DC: 7.148 MKD (0.1161 EUR) incl. taxes. No licence stated. Eurostat data is reusable under its standard reuse policy.
- Confidence: medium (official VAT pages conflict; the per-kWh delta is derived from averages).

## BA - Bosnia and Herzegovina
- VAT: 17%, a single rate across all of BiH with no reduced rates. (source: https://www.uino.gov.ba/portal/bs/pdv/opste-informacije-o-sistemu-pdv-a?print=print ; Eurostat ratio 1.17)
- Changes since 2025-01-01 (three regulated public suppliers):
  - ERS (Republika Srpska), 2025-01-01: +7.9%. https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/poskupljenje-struje-republika-srpska-1-januar-2026/33491028.html
  - EPBiH (FBiH), 2025-09-01: FERK approved a new 3-block model with a +10.04% average household increase. Energy component: Green (0-350 kWh) 15.90 f/kWh, Blue (350-1000 kWh) 16.20, Red (>1000 kWh) 16.50. Network tariffs unchanged. https://mojaenergija.epbih.ba/novosti/ferk-odobrio-nove-cijene-kupci-epbih-i-dalje-placaju-najnizu-cijenu-elektricne-energije/ ; https://forbes.n1info.ba/ekonomija/kako-novo-poskupljenje-struje-u-fbih-pravi-razliku-medju-gradjanima-istog-entiteta/
  - EP HZHB (FBiH, Herzegovina): tariffs unchanged. https://forbes.n1info.ba/ekonomija/kako-novo-poskupljenje-struje-u-fbih-pravi-razliku-medju-gradjanima-istog-entiteta/
  - ERS (RS), 2026-02-01: RERS raised network tariffs for 2026-2028 by about 20%, so household bills rise about +10%. https://poslovnenovine.ba/2025/12/22/struja-u-rs-poskupljuje-od-1-februara-2026-gradanima-racuni-veci-za-10-privredi-za-6 ; https://balkangreenenergynews.com/rs/u-2026-poskupljuje-struja-u-bih-severnoj-makedoniji-i-srbiji/
  - FBiH connection fees changed on 2026-03-01 (239 KM/kW LV). This is not a per-kWh charge.
  - Net national effect: the DC-band price was +6.5% year on year in H1 2026 (16.40 KM/100 kWh excl. taxes), about +0.010 BAM/kWh excl. VAT versus 2025-S1 (approximate).
- Spot exposure: regulated - households are on public-supply regulated tariffs in all three utility areas.
- Household all-in price: 0.1946 BAM/kWh incl. all taxes and VAT (DC band 2500-5000 kWh, H1 2026), from the Agency for Statistics of BiH, reported in https://energetika.ba/novosti/cijene-elektricne-energije-u-bih-porasle-u-prvom-polugodistu/26803. Eurostat 2025-S2: 0.189 BAM (0.0966 EUR). No licence stated.
- Confidence: medium (three utilities move separately; the national delta is approximate; one RFE line mentions a "+6.85% FBiH from 2026-09-01" that I could not verify).

## AL - Albania
- VAT: 20% standard rate, which applies to household electricity. A 700 kWh bill is 5,950 ALL excl. VAT and 7,140 ALL incl. VAT. (sources: https://english.gazetatema.net/economy/electricity-price-cut-for-over-a-million-households-in-albania-i338366 (search result); Eurostat I_TAX/X_VAT 11.4/9.5 = 1.20; https://www.vatupdate.com/2021/08/30/vat-rates-in-albania-2/)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - 2025-02-01: ERE cut the household end-user tariff from 9.5 to 8.5 ALL/kWh excl. VAT for consumption up to 700 kWh/month (over 1M subscribers, about 95% of OSHEE customers). Above 700 kWh stays at 9.5. https://oshee.al/vendimi-oshee-aplikon-8-5-lek-cmimin-e-energjise-elektrike-per-konsumin-deri-ne-700-kwh-perfitojne-mbi-1-mln-abonente/
  - 2026: ERE extended the 8.5/9.5 tariff through 2026-12-31. https://rtsh.al/nuk-ndryshon-cmimi-i-energjise-per-2026-energjia-elektrike-per-fashen-deri-700-kilovat-me-8-5-leke/
  - Distribution tariff unchanged for 2026-05-01 to 12-31 (average 5.89 ALL/kWh; 0.4 kV 6.42), bundled in the end tariff. https://scantv.al/english/lajme/shqiperia/tarifat-e-shperndarjes-se-energjise-elektrike-ere-miraton-vendim-i30864
  - Note: Eurostat still reports 9.5 ALL excl. VAT (11.4 incl.) for 2025-S1 and 2025-S2, so the baseline appears to miss the cut. Whether the 2022 ">800 kWh at 42 ALL" tier survives is not verified (the 2025 decision describes only 700/9.5).
- Spot exposure: regulated - OSHEE universal-service fixed tariff.
- Household all-in price: 10.2 ALL/kWh incl. VAT (8.5 x 1.2, for up to 700 kWh/month, which covers the DC band), valid 2025-02-01 to 2026-12-31. Sources: OSHEE/ERE as above. Eurostat 2025-S2: 11.4 ALL (0.1175 EUR), which looks stale. No licence stated.
- Confidence: medium (tariff level verified; Eurostat discrepancy unexplained; VAT not confirmed on the tax authority site).

## XK - Kosovo
- VAT: 8% reduced rate on electricity supply, including transmission and distribution (standard rate 18%). (sources: https://www.atk-ks.org/en/portfolio/informata-te-pergjithshme-per-tatimet-ne-kosove/ (search result); https://www.pwc.com/ks/en/Kosovo_2022_TaxSummary.pdf (search result); Eurostat ratio 0.0877/0.0812 = 1.08)
- Changes since 2025-01-01:
  - 2025-05-01: ERO approved a +16.1% increase across all categories (decision 2025-04-16). Households pay 7.79 EURc/kWh up to 800 kWh/month and 14.45 EURc/kWh above. https://prishtinainsight.com/kosovo-energy-regulator-determines-electricity-tariffs-after-detested-price-hike/ ; ERO decision https://www.ero-ks.org/zrre/sites/default/files/Publikimet/Vendimet/Vendimet%202025/3.%20Decision%20on%20Tariffs_2025_USS-FINAL.pdf (effective "from 01 May 2025")
  - 2026 (ERO approval about May 2026): KESCO end-user tariffs unchanged. ERO rejected KESCO's request for over 20%. The KEDS distribution tariff fell 2.7% on average, but the consumer price was unchanged. https://zeri.info/ekonomia/621834/zrre-ja-miraton-tarifat-per-kostt-in-keds-in-dhe-kesco-n-pa-ndryshime-ne-cmimin-e-energjise-per-vitin-2026/
  - Net: Eurostat DC excl. VAT went from 0.0709 (2025-S1) to 0.0812 (2025-S2), about +0.010 EUR/kWh, already partly in a 2025 baseline.
- Spot exposure: regulated - households are on KESCO universal-service regulated tariffs.
- Household all-in price: 0.0877 EUR/kWh incl. VAT (Eurostat nrg_pc_204 DC, 2025-S2). Eurostat covers XK. Still valid in 2026 since tariffs are unchanged. Eurostat reuse policy (CC BY 4.0).
- Confidence: medium (exact tariff table and VAT-inclusion not read from the PDF annex; 2026 effective date approximate).

## UA - Ukraine
- VAT: 20%, already included in the fixed price (3.60 + 0.72 VAT = 4.32 UAH/kWh). (source: CMU Resolution No. 483 of 2019-06-05, annex "Фіксовані ціни", https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/483-2019-%D0%BF)
- Changes since 2025-01-01: none to the household price. The fixed price of 4.32 UAH/kWh incl. VAT has applied since 2024-06-01. The PSO was extended repeatedly (to 2025-10-31, 2026-04-30, then 2026-10-31). The reduced heating rate is 2.64 UAH (2.20 + 0.44 VAT) for October-April up to 2000 kWh/month. Two-zone meters get a night rate of 2.16 UAH. Ministry of Economy forecasts of 4.75-5.18 UAH in 2026 have not been legislated. The resolution expires 2026-10-31; no decision on November onward was found. https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/483-2019-%D0%BF ; https://unn.ua/en/news/in-ukraine-the-electricity-tariff-for-the-population-will-remain-unchanged-until-november ; https://agroreview.com/en/newsen/electricity-tariff-for-households-will/
- Spot exposure: regulated - the PSO fixes the household price, and a spot-based estimate would be meaningless.
- Household all-in price: 4.32 UAH/kWh incl. VAT, valid 2024-06-01 to 2026-10-31 (official legal act, CMU Res. 483). Not in Eurostat nrg_pc_204 for 2024-S1 onward. Licence: official legislative text (public).
- Confidence: high for the price and VAT. Watch the 2026-10-31 expiry.

## MD - Moldova
- VAT: 0%. Household electricity is VAT-exempt with deduction right (zero-rated in effect), and ANRE prices are quoted "fără TVA". A government draft (2026-09, awaiting Parliament) would apply 0% to the first 100 kWh/month and 20% above, from 2027-04-01. It has not been adopted. (sources: https://anre.md/anre-a-aprobat-noi-preturi-reglementate-pentru-energia-electrica-3-1287 ; https://unimedia.info/ro/news/20a2c8a7d07b98b7/moldovenii-care-vor-depasi-plafoanele-instituite-de-guvern-la-consumul-de-gaz-si-electricitate-vor-plati-facturi-mai-mari-din-aprilie-tva-va-creste-la-20.html ; https://ziar.md/tva-diferit-in-functie-de-consum-cum-ar-putea-fi-taxate-facturile-la-lumina-si-gazul-natural-din-2027/ ; Eurostat I_TAX = X_VAT)
- Changes since 2025-01-01 (ANRE regulated LV household prices; ANRE quotes these in bani/kWh, converted here to lei, 100 bani = 1 leu):
  - ANRE decision 2025-01-03, effective on publication: Premier Energy 2.57 -> 4.10 lei/kWh; FEE Nord 3.20 -> 4.68 lei/kWh (Transnistria/gas crisis). https://anre.md/anre-a-ajustat-preturile-si-tarifele-pentru-energia-termica-si-energia-electrica-3-981 (previous level: https://realitatea.md/doc-anre-a-anuntat-noile-preturi-la-energie-electrica-premier-energy-257-lei-kwh-fee-nord-320-lei-kwh/)
  - 2025-08-01: Premier Energy -> 3.59 lei/kWh; FEE Nord -> 4.00 lei/kWh. The source says "about -13%/-14% from about 3.91/4.34", which implies an intermediate 2025 step I did not verify. https://renergy.md/ultima-ora-curentul-se-ieftineste-cu-pana-la-14-anre-reduce-tarifele-premier-energy-si-fee-nord-pentru-2025/
  - April 2026 (ANRE decision 2026-03-31): Premier Energy 3.59 -> 3.56 lei/kWh; FEE Nord 4.00 -> 3.95 lei/kWh. Time-of-use pricing introduced. Suppliers had asked for 3.83 and 4.32. https://anre.md/anre-a-aprobat-noi-preturi-reglementate-pentru-energia-electrica-3-1287 ; https://www.moldpres.md/rom/economie/anre-a-ieftinit-energia-electrica-pentru-consumatorii-premier-energy-si-fee-nord-noile-tarife-in-vigoare-din-aprilie
  - Compensation: "Ajutor la contor" via the Energy Vulnerability Reduction Fund. In the 2025-26 season about 622k families received help covering about 40% of the bill, with category-based compensated prices. https://ecofm.md/2026/04/03/circa-622-000-de-familii-vulnerabile-beneficiaza-de-compensatii-pentru-energie-ministra-natalia-plugaru-ajutorul-acopera-40-din-costul-facturii/
  - Net for Premier Energy since 2025-01-01: +0.99 lei/kWh. But relative to the Eurostat 2025 baseline (2025-S1 4.20 lei, 2025-S2 3.87 lei), the current price of about 3.56-3.95 lei is lower.
- Spot exposure: regulated - households are on ANRE-regulated universal-service prices.
- Household all-in price: 3.56 MDL/kWh (Premier Energy, centre/south, about 2/3 of customers) and 3.95 MDL/kWh (FEE Nord), VAT 0%, from April 2026, ANRE. Eurostat covers MD (2025-S2 DC 3.87 MDL, 0.1967 EUR). No licence stated.
- Confidence: medium (exact effective dates are "on publication in Monitorul Oficial"; an intermediate 2025 step is unverified).
