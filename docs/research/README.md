# Research

The research behind the prices in `config/`. Each folder is a dated snapshot:
what was asked, what was found, the arithmetic, and the sources. Prices and
rules have moved on since; the config tables and their `note` columns are the
current state.

| Folder | Date | Question |
|---|---|---|
| [`tariffs/`](tariffs/) | 2026-10-04 | Europe's live countries: the ENTSO-E API, add-ons, VAT, zone weights, exchange rates |
| [`zoned-countries/`](zoned-countries/) | 2026-10-04 | Per-region prices for the United States, Canada and Russia |
| [`rest-of-world/`](rest-of-world/) | 2026-10-04 | One published household price for every other country |
| [`zones-and-averages/`](zones-and-averages/) | 2026-10-05 | Zones for Kazakhstan, Iraq, Somalia and St Kitts and Nevis; a second look at the places left on an average |

Most of the research was done by several researchers working in parallel, one
topic or region each, following a written method (`method.md` where there is
one). Bulky downloads (source PDFs, saved pages) are not kept here; every
figure cites its URL.
