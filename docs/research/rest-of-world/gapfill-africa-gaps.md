# africa-gaps — household electricity prices (researched 2026-10-04)

Group: BI CF DJ ER ET GQ KM MG MW MZ SD SO SS TD. All 14 priced; four rows are
averages or dated grids rather than current residential schedules (flagged low).

## BI — Burundi (REGIDESO)

- Sept 2017 revision (iwacu-burundi.org, 15/09/2017): first block 0–50 kWh raised 68 → 82 FBu/kWh;
  REGIDESO's own example: 150 kWh/month = 33,100 FBu → **220.67 FBu/kWh**. Implied 51–150 rate
  ≈ (33,100 − 50×82)/100 = 290 FBu/kWh, but the full grid above 50 kWh was never published.
- The World Bank Burundi National Energy Compact (2026) states the **2017 tariff structure is still in
  force** ("Structure tarifaire de 2017 en cours d'actualisation", avg 9.4 USc/kWh — consistent:
  220.67 FBu ≈ 7.6 USc). regideso.bi/tarifs renders only a heading, no data. The older 1 June 2012 grid
  (93/149/190 FBu per bimestre blocks) is superseded.
- Consumption stated at 150 kWh/month because that is the utility's own all-in example; at 200 kWh the
  price would need the unpublished next block. Low confidence.

## CF — Central African Republic (ENERCA)

- The method's preference for a current ENERCA tariff could not be satisfied: enerca-rca.com now
  301-redirects to an unrelated Indian site (domain lapsed/squatted); ARSEC (regulator) has no web
  presence; no CAR press article with a tariff grid was found (searches in French across
  radiondekeluka/oubanguimedias/corbeaunews etc.); the APUA utility profile lists contacts only.
- Kept the previous pass's figure: **83 XAF/kWh**, the 2017 all-customer average effective tariff from the
  World Bank CAR Emergency Electricity Supply and Access Project PAD (2019; the documents.worldbank.org
  file is mislabelled "SIERRA-LEONE" but the content is CAR). The 2022 ESSAP PAD repeats US$0.14/kWh and
  notes many customers are billed flat-rate by hours of use. Low confidence, not residential-specific.

## DJ — Djibouti (EDD)

- Full EDD tariff arrêté of 6 February 2012 (signed Guelleh), retrieved as PDF (theiguides.org copy;
  edd.dj refused connections at research time but its billing pages use the same tariff codes).
- Residential BT codes: Social 1 (code 1, PS 1 kVA): 27 FD/kWh first 200 kWh, 63 FD after, prime fixe
  544 FD/month → 200 kWh = 5,944 FD = 29.72 FD/kWh. **Social 2 (code 15, PS 3 kVA, taken as typical):
  200×40 + 951 = 8,951 FD → 44.76 FD/kWh.** Domestique (code 2, ≥6 kVA): 55/58 FD + 1,069 FD/month →
  300 kWh = 59.6 FD/kWh. Regions (code 7): 40/58 FD + 540 FD/month.
- Prepaid: code 21 (1 kVA) 31 FD/kWh flat; code 22 53 FD/kWh flat, no prime fixe.
- No newer arrêté found; a 2020 "tariff reduction" article (djibstyle) is offline. Medium confidence.

## ER — Eritrea (EEC)

- No residential schedule is published anywhere findable. AfDB Dekemhare 30 MW Solar PV project
  appraisal report: **average consumption tariff ERN 2.550/kWh (~17 US¢ at the official 15 ERN/USD)**,
  below the ~20 US¢ production cost, state-set and "not expected to change". Climatescope 2025 reports
  the average price unchanged since 2023. Low confidence; average across all classes, no block detail.

## ET — Ethiopia (EEU)

- The 2024–2028 reform raises tariffs in 16 quarterly steps. Latest quarter with a fully published
  residential band table: **Apr–Jun 2025** (ethiodiasporahub reporting the EEU schedule): 0–50 kWh 0.60,
  51–100 1.49, 101–200 2.67, 201–300 3.84 birr/kWh; prepaid service charge 4.18 birr (postpaid 10.95).
- 200 kWh: 50×0.60 + 50×1.49 + 100×2.67 = 371.50 + 4.18 = 375.68; ×1.155 (15% VAT + 0.5% regulatory
  fee) = 433.91; + 10 birr TV licence = 443.91 → **2.22 birr/kWh**.
- Cross-checks: Jul–Oct 2025 increments (birrmetrics, 20/08/2025: +0.0807 on 0–50, +0.4596 ≈ 12% on
  201–300) match these bases; Oct 11–Dec 30 2025 published endpoints (capitalethiopia, 19/10/2025):
  0.7571 (≤50) and 5.0990 (>500) birr — i.e. ~+25% over Apr–Jun in two quarters. Quarterly rises of
  ~11–12% continued into 2026 (8th step June 2026 per energyforgrowth), so the actual Oct 2026 price is
  plausibly ~3.2–3.5 birr/kWh all-in at 200 kWh, but no current full table is published (EEU's site still
  shows the old Dec 2018–Dec 2021 schedule). Marked low for that staleness.

## GQ — Equatorial Guinea (SEGESA)

- Ley No 3/2002 tariff still applied (press through 2025 reports the replacement tariff law adopted by
  parliament but not yet implemented; an ex-SEGESA director calls the applied rates "illegal since 2013",
  i.e. unchanged). Malabo/Bata low-tension, hydro-supplied: 1–250 kWh at 60 XAF → 200 kWh =
  **60 XAF/kWh**. Diesel-supplied variant: 1–150 at 60 + 151–250 at 90 → 67.5 XAF/kWh at 200 kWh.
  Some customers instead get a social flat rate of 1,500 XAF/month for ≤250 kWh (realequatorialguinea).
  Rural independent-producer rates 55–80 XAF/kWh. Low confidence; dated law, uneven application.

## KM — Comoros (SONELEC)

- IMF Country Report No 24/5 (Jan 2024, French): tariff raised **132 → 198 KMF/kWh on 6 June 2022**
  (flat; still below ~350 KMF/kWh production cost). No later revision found. 200 kWh → 198 KMF/kWh.
  Medium confidence; fixed charges, if any, not identified.

## MG — Madagascar (JIRAMA)

- Regulator ORE page (ore.mg): Optima Résidentiel in force since June 2021; the Nov 2024 revision
  changed Business tariffs only. Tarif 20 "Economique" (PS ≤3.3 kW, zones 1=2): T1 ≤130 kWh 340 Ar,
  131–300 kWh 560 Ar, >300 764 Ar; prime fixe 3,018 Ar/kW/month; redevance 8,000 Ar/month.
- 200 kWh, 2.2 kW subscribed: 130×340 + 70×560 = 83,400 + 6,640 + 8,000 = 98,040 Ar; ×1.20 TVA =
  117,648 → **588 Ar/kWh** (568 with 1.1 kW subscribed; 20% TVA is the standard rate — its application
  is the main untested assumption). Tarif 10 social is worse at 200 kWh (50×130 + 150×764).

## MW — Malawi (ESCOM/MERA)

- ET1 domestic prepaid from 1 Feb 2025 (16% MERA tranche of the 2023–27 base-tariff path): first
  50 kWh 79.90 MWK, above 122.25 MWK. 200 kWh: 3,995 + 18,337.50 = 22,332.50 → **111.66 MWK/kWh**.
- escom.mw timed out at research time; rates cross-confirmed via times.mw / mwnation / businessmalawi
  coverage. The approved path adds 12% (2025/26) and 9% (2026/27), but ESCOM posted a suspension of the
  residential hike and no implementation announcement was found, so Feb 2025 rates are kept. Postpaid
  ET2 is slightly cheaper (70.70/115.65 + fixed charge). Medium confidence.

## MZ — Mozambique (EDM)

- Current table (energypedia, updated Apr 2025, matching EDM's grid): tarifa social 1.03 MZN/kWh
  (low-consumption/limited-power customers, no fixed fee); tarifa doméstica flat **7.58 MZN/kWh +
  257.97 MZN/month** taxa fixa (the older 6.63/9.39/9.85 block structure is superseded).
- 200 kWh doméstica: 1,516 + 257.97 = 1,773.97; ×1.16 IVA = 2,057.81 → **10.29 MZN/kWh** (8.87 if
  IVA-exempt — EDM's Dec 2024 order exempting *social* customers from IVA implies doméstica carries
  16% IVA). EDM's own sites were unreachable; medium confidence.

## SD — Sudan (SEDC)

- Applied silently from 1 May 2026 (akhbarsakhina 03/05, alnilin 02/05 — consistent, ~72% rise, no
  official statement): first 100 kWh 7,000 SDG (70 SDG/kWh, was 40), second 100 kWh 9,000 (90, was 50),
  third 100 kWh 11,000 (110, was 60). 200 kWh = 16,000 SDG → **80 SDG/kWh**. Wartime volatility and
  unannounced changes make this low confidence despite being 5 months old.

## SO — Somalia (private utilities)

- No national tariff. BGS "Navigating Non-Technical Barriers to Affordable Electricity in Somalia"
  (Oct 2025; 2023 data): national average **$0.81/kWh**; Mogadishu (BECO) lowest at **$0.36** ($0.41
  during the 2022 fuel spike); Jubaland $0.90; Puntland/Galmudug/Hirshabelle ~$1.00. Mogadishu used as
  the single figure (largest service area). Billed in USD. Low confidence (survey averages, not a
  published schedule; BECO also runs declining blocks per the same report).

## SS — South Sudan (JEDCO, Juba)

- From 8 Apr 2025 (13% reduction, sudanspost): residential ≤100 kWh **$0.303/kWh**, >100 kWh
  **$0.316/kWh**, USD-denominated, prepaid. 200 kWh → $61.90 → **$0.31/kWh**. Grid service essentially
  Juba only. Medium confidence.

## TD — Chad (SNE)

- SNE basse tension domestic grid (tchadinfos, Apr 2020, quoting SNE): 0–150 kWh 85 XAF/kWh, >150 kWh
  125 XAF/kWh. 200 kWh = 19,000 XAF → **95 XAF/kWh**. No later revision found; SNE publishes nothing
  online. Fixed charges/TVA not itemised in the source. Low confidence.

## Currency

- **SS:** tariffs are set and quoted in **USD**; households pay in SSP converted at the prevailing
  market rate at vending time (sudanspost.com JEDCO coverage; eyeradio.org). CSV row kept in USD.
- **SO:** private utilities bill in **USD** (de facto dollarised retail electricity; BGS report quotes
  all prices in USD). CSV row in USD.
- **ER:** households are billed in **ERN** (nakfa); the official exchange rate is fixed at 15 ERN/USD,
  the parallel rate is several times that, so USD conversions overstate the burden (AfDB PAR).
- **SD:** billed in **SDG** (prepaid vending in SDG; news tariffs quoted in SDG).
- Others are unambiguous: BIF, XAF (CF/GQ/TD, CEMAC franc), DJF (pegged to USD), ETB, KMF (pegged to
  EUR at 491.97), MGA, MWK, MZN.

## Zone candidates

- **SO Somalia:** Mogadishu/Banadir $0.36 vs Jubaland $0.90 and Puntland / Galmudug / Hirshabelle
  ~$1.00/kWh (2023, BGS report) — ~2.5–2.8×, and the federal member states are a short recognised list.
  Strongest zone candidate in this group.
- **GQ:** hydro-grid vs diesel-grid cities differ (60 vs 67.5 XAF/kWh at 200 kWh) and rural
  independent-producer rates reach 80 XAF/kWh — under 2×, not proposed as zones.
- No other country in the group has a published ≥2× regional household split (BI/DJ/MG grids have
  region columns but residential rates are equal or within ~45%).

## Gaps

- None unpriced, but four rows are not current residential schedules and are flagged **low**:
  **CF** (2017 World Bank all-customer average; ENERCA's web presence is gone and no current grid is
  published anywhere found), **ER** (state-set average, no schedule published), **GQ** (2002-law rates
  still applied pending an unimplemented new tariff law), **ET** (latest *fully published* band table is
  Apr–Jun 2025; quarterly ~11–12% rises continued into 2026, so the figure understates today's bill —
  current-quarter table simply is not published; EEU's site still shows the 2018–2021 schedule).
- **BI** is priced at REGIDESO's own 150 kWh example because the blocks above 50 kWh were never
  published in full.
