# world-gaps: household electricity price per kWh (gap-fill, researched 2026-10-04)

Group: HT VE YE NU PW TV WF LA MX — the countries the regional passes could not finish.
Figures are all-in per kWh at the stated monthly consumption, in the billing currency. Rows and sources: `world-gaps.csv`.

## HT Haiti: 22.30 HTG/kWh (200 kWh/month, medium)

EDH raised residential rates from the 2009-era average of 9.57 HTG/kWh to 15.93 (January 2023 bills) and then 22.30 HTG/kWh (bills from April 2023); the US Country Commercial Guide records the revision (residential ~$0.07 → ~$0.17) and it is the last known change through October 2026. Reported as a single per-class rate, so 200 × 22.30 = 4,460 HTG. The 2009 arrêté grid (1–30 kWh 4.80, 31–200 5.10, 201+ 12.74, subscription 150 HTG/month — Wayback copy of edh.ht) is superseded; whether the 150 HTG subscription survives is unverified (it would make the effective price 23.05). TCA (10%) treatment on bills is unresolved; EDH historically quoted averages "TTC". Caveat that dwarfs the price: Péligre has been offline since June 2025 and metropolitan Port-au-Prince supply is near zero to a few hours/day — 22.30 is what is billed when there is anything to bill.
Sources: https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/haiti-energy , http://web.archive.org/web/20130406060411/http://www.edh.ht:80/frais-tarif.php , https://lenouvelliste.com/article/244196/ledh-opte-pour-une-nouvelle-grille-tarifaire (paywalled), https://haitieconomie.com/haiti-lelectricite-rare-chere-et-instable-un-frein-majeur-au-developpement/

## VE Venezuela: ~20 VES/kWh (300 kWh/month, low)

No pliego tarifario has been published in the Gaceta Oficial for the 2024–2026 increases — Corpoelec itself pointed users to a Gaceta "not yet published" (El Diario, Dec 2024). Billing is quasi-flat per household category (Jan 2024 reference: Residencial Social $1.16/month, General $2.17, Alto Consumo $4.34, billed in Bs at the official rate), meters largely unread, and bills rise every month tracking devaluation. Hard evidence (El Pitazo, April 2026 bills): Caracas 3,700 Bs, Barquisimeto 8,970 Bs → 12.3–29.9 VES/kWh at an assumed 300 kWh; the CSV carries the midpoint ~20 with as_of 2026-04. Scaling by the BCV rate (487 → 871 VES/USD) implies ~22–53 VES/kWh by October 2026, stated as a trajectory, not asserted as the price. A circulating tiered "pliego" (T-01/T-02/T-03 on luz.com.ve etc.) is uncorroborated and internally inconsistent; not used. OVSP, the usual survey source, is offline with no 2025–2026 bulletin found. Households additionally spend $20–180/month on blackout coping (Cedice via Tal Cual).
Sources: https://elpitazo.net/economia/la-luz-aumenta-y-el-servicio-empeora-testimonios-revelan-incrementos-mensuales-en-la-electricidad/ (fetched via Wayback 2026-05-04), https://eldiario.com/2024/12/10/supuesto-aumento-tarifas-residenciales-corpoelec/ , https://caraotadigital.net/venezuela/denunciaron-aumento-excesivo-en-las-tarifas-de-corpoelec-esto-es-lo-que-se-sabe/ , https://talcualdigital.com/el-costo-de-los-apagones-venezolanos-gastan-entre-20-y-180-dolares-en-un-mes/ , https://finanzasdigital.com/tasa-de-cambio-bcv-05-octubre-2026/

## YE Yemen: 19 YER/kWh grid tariff (200 kWh/month, low) — two-number reality

The Ministry of Electricity's July 2026 memorandum (published by South24) states the current residential tariff is 19 YER/kWh, unchanged for consumption ≤1,000 kWh/month (a proposed rise to 700 YER/kWh applies above 1,000 and to commercial; approval status unclear). This matches the IGC's 2020/21 PEC Aden billing average (19 YER/kWh vs production cost 256). The tariff is largely symbolic: collection ~20–66%, supply a few hours/day. What many households actually buy is private-generator power: the Sana'a authorities set 257 YER/kWh (November 2023, diesel-indexed, in Sana'a "old" rials ≈ US$0.48); Taiz generators reached 400 YER/kWh in 2021. 200 × 19 = 3,800 YER (Aden rials) is the grid bill; treat the figure as grid-tariff-only with the generator reality in the note.
Sources: https://south24.net/news/newse.php?nid=5623 , https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2021/11/Improving-electricity-services-in-Yemen_Final-report.pdf , https://sanaacenter.org/the-yemen-review/sept-oct-2023/21234 , https://www.saba.ye/en/news3281568.htm

## NU Niue: 0.55 NZD/kWh (200 kWh/month, low)

Niue Power Corporation tiers per the Niue Strategic Energy Road Map 2015–2025: first 100 kWh 0.50, 101–300 0.60, >300 0.70 NZD. 100 × 0.50 + 100 × 0.60 = NZ$110 / 200 = 0.55. No fixed charge documented. Heavily subsidised (~NZ$2M/yr; NiSERM estimates true cost NZ$2.70/kWh). The 12.5% Niue Consumption Tax does not list electricity among exempt supplies, but whether published tiers are NCT-inclusive is unstated. Low confidence because NPC has no website or published rates page, did not report to the PPA 2023 benchmarking, and no tariff act since 2015 could be found — the structure may have changed unannounced.
Sources: https://policy.asiapacificenergy.org/node/2695 (PDF: https://policy.asiapacificenergy.org/sites/default/files/Niue%20Strategic%20Energy%20Road%20Map%202015%E2%80%932025.pdf), https://mof.gov.nu/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Niue-NCT-Act-2009.pdf

## PW Palau: 0.3465 USD/kWh (200 kWh/month, medium)

PPUC schedule effective 1 October 2024 (official notice, reproduced in full with the rate table by Island Times): residential base rate 0.051 (0–150 kWh) / 0.125 (151–500) / 0.170 (501+) plus the Distributed Energy Rate of 0.277/kWh (the fuel/solar pass-through that replaced the AFPAC surcharge). 150 × 0.328 + 50 × 0.402 = $69.30 / 200 = 0.3465 — identical to PPUC's own estimated-charges table and consistent with the PPA FY2023 benchmark ($69.55). The RPPL 11-39 subsidy (−$0.10/kWh ≤500 kWh) expired 1 April 2025, so this unsubsidised schedule is what households pay. PGST 10%: PPUC sought restored tax-exempt status, outcome unresolved; its bill table shows no PGST line. April 2026 press expected a DER-driven rise in May 2026 — not confirmed, so the figure may be slightly stale.
Sources: https://islandtimes.org/ppuc-to-reduce-power-rates-with-new-distributed-energy-rate-system/ (table: https://islandtimes.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/rates.jpg), https://islandtimes.org/palau-utility-customers-face-higher-bills-as-subsidies-expire/ , https://islandtimes.org/palau-faces-power-price-dilemma-as-fuel-costs-rise-subsidy-debate-intensifies/ , https://www.ppa.org.fj/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/PPA-Benchmarking-Report-2023-Final-250925.pdf

## TV Tuvalu: 0.4525 AUD/kWh (200 kWh/month, medium)

TEC residential tariff, Fongafale/Funafuti, from the utility's own live site: first 50 kWh A$0.30, next 50 A$0.39, above 100 A$0.56 (outer islands 0.29/0.38/0.55 → 0.4425). 50 × 0.30 + 50 × 0.39 + 100 × 0.56 = A$90.50 / 200. Prepaid meters, no fixed charge found. Legislated January 2008 and unchanged since (ADB sector assessment); well below cost (~A$0.82/kWh, ~A$1M/yr subsidy). Consumption tax (7%): the first 50 kWh of domestic supply per month is explicitly exempt; whether the posted prices are CT-inclusive above that is unstated. Note tec.tv is a parked domain — the real site is tectuvalu.tv; the tariff page dates from 2021, which is why this is medium, not high.
Sources: https://www.tectuvalu.tv/residential/ (table: https://www.tectuvalu.tv/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Residential-tariff.jpg), https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/linked-documents/49450-015-ssa.pdf , https://tuvalu-legislation.tv/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2009/2009-0007/2009-0007_2.pdf

## WF Wallis and Futuna: ~19.1 XPF/kWh (200 kWh/month, low)

Since 1 January 2020 EEWF residential customers pay the metropolitan Tarif Bleu résidentiel Base "en niveau et en structure" (arrêté of 29 June 2016; CRE délibération 2020-002), with CSPE and local/territorial taxes folded into the TTC level. The IEOM 2024 annual report publishes the EEWF series: 16.4 (Dec 2022) → 20.9 (Dec 2023) → 22.5 F CFP/kWh (through Dec 2024), and records the −15% TRV cut of 1 February 2025 → ≈19.1. 200 × 19.1 ≈ 3,820 XPF energy-only; the monthly abonnement (per-kVA standing charge) is excluded because its amount could not be found — eewf.engie.com refuses connections and was never archived. The territorial 3.59 F/kWh electricity tax is believed inside the TTC level per the alignment formula, unproven. Metropolitan TRV movements after Feb 2025 (e.g. the August 2025 adjustment) would carry over; not quantified here.
Sources: https://www.ieom.fr/IMG/pdf/rapport-annuel-wf-2024-2.pdf , https://www.cre.fr/content/download/21893/278075 , https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053459721

## LA Laos: 1,178 LAK/kWh (200 kWh/month, high)

The gap the earlier pass left — EDL's middle tiers — is closed from EDL's own published 2026 residential table (0.4 kV), fetched from the edl.com.la price API and verified by eye (the image is not kept here). Rates escalate **monthly**; October 2026 column: 0–25 kWh 828, **26–150 kWh 932**, **151–300 kWh 1,539**, 301–400 1,668, 401–500 1,801, 501–1,000 1,855, 1,001–1,500 1,934, >1,500 2,016 LAK/kWh. Legal basis printed on the table: MoIC/MEM agreement 1990 (09 Sep 2025), PM Office notice 062 (14 May 2026), EDL notice 1916 (19 Apr 2026). At 200 kWh: 25 × 828 + 125 × 932 + 50 × 1,539 = 214,150; + 10% VAT (applies to residential electricity; a mooted exemption was not approved as of Aug 2026) = 235,565 LAK → 1,177.8 LAK/kWh. The small monthly meter-maintenance fee (3,346 LAK in 2015, current value unknown, order of +2%) is excluded. Press cross-checks (Laotian Times, Asia News Network) match the Aug and Dec columns exactly. November and December 2026 step up again (Nov: 839/945/1,565 → ~1,196 all-in).
Sources: https://edl-inside-api.edl.com.la/prices/svc-0d4b0496-a9cb-4bab-913a-3bf577884713.jpg (primary), https://edl-inside-api.edl.com.la/api_v1/admin-svc/prices/get , https://www.edl.com.la/price , https://laotiantimes.com/2026/08/10/laos-considers-vat-exemption-to-subsidize-electricity-for-low-income-households/ , https://asianews.network/electricity-prices-surge-in-laos-amid-rising-demand-and-weakened-kip/ , https://rise.esmap.org/sites/default/files/library/lao-pdr/Electricity%20Pricing%20in%20the%20residential%20sector.pdf

## MX Mexico: 2.88 MXN/kWh (Tarifa 1, 250 kWh/month, medium)

CFE Tarifa 1 (standard domestic, temperate), October 2026: básico first 75 kWh 1.140, intermedio next 65 kWh 1.385, excedente 4.054 MXN/kWh; no fixed charge (a 25 kWh minimum charge is new for 2026); DAC threshold 250 kWh/month (12-month average). 75 × 1.140 + 65 × 1.385 + 110 × 4.054 = 621.47 + 16% IVA = 720.90 MXN / 250 = 2.88. Billing is bimonthly for most customers (blocks double per bill). Caveats: (a) 250 kWh/month sits exactly on the DAC threshold — a household sustaining it is reclassified to DAC (Oct 2026 Central: 145.79 fixed + 6.730/kWh flat → eff. ~8.48 MXN/kWh), so 2.88 is the top of the subsidised range; (b) typical temperate-zone use is often quoted ~250 kWh per **bimester** (125/month), which gives 1.44 MXN/kWh — the blended price is extremely consumption-sensitive; (c) the DAP municipal street-lighting charge (varies, on-bill) is excluded; border municipalities pay 8% IVA. Sourcing: domestic tariffs 1–1F are set by SHCP (DOF 31 Dec 2025 authorization, código 5777718; 2026 cuotas via Oficio 349-B-1-069, deliberately not DOF-published — the monthly DOF "Tarifas Finales" notes carry only the unsubsidised DB1/DB2 schedule). CFE's own tariff pages are WAF-blocked (403) and Wayback copies need a form postback, so the figures rest on two independent CFE-citing trackers plus a press-corroborated +0.311%/month chain from June 2026 — hence medium, not high.
Sources: https://airegulasolutions.com/cfe/tarifas?year=2026&month=10&segment=DOMESTIC , https://appcfecontigo.com.mx/cfe-tarifas/ , https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle.php?codigo=5800039&fecha=30/09/2026 , https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle.php?codigo=5777718&fecha=31/12/2025 , https://www.unotv.com/nacional/las-tarifas-domesticas-de-cfe-van-de-1-a-4-pesos-por-kwh-en-junio-de-2026/ , https://www.infobae.com/mexico/2026/05/14/tarifa-de-verano-cfe-2026-como-saber-si-tu-recibo-de-luz-tiene-subsidio-por-altas-temperaturas/

## Currency

- **VE**: VES (bolívar digital). Corpoelec's category fees are set in USD terms and invoiced in VES at the **BCV official rate** — use it for conversions (871.37 VES/USD on 2026-10-05; parallel ~962, gap only ~11%; a year earlier the gap was much wider, so date any historical conversion). https://www.bcv.org.ve/seccionportal/tipo-de-cambio-oficial-del-bcv
- **YE**: YER, but two divergent rials. Aden "new" rials (CBY-Aden, floating): ~1,520–1,630/USD through 2026. Sana'a "old" rials (pegged): ~535/USD. The 19 YER grid tariff is in Aden rials (≈ US$0.012); the 257 YER generator tariff is in Sana'a rials (≈ US$0.48). Never convert one with the other's rate. https://www.yemenmonitor.com/en/Details/ArtMID/908/ArticleID/164362
- **NU**: NZD (Niue uses the New Zealand dollar). **PW**: USD. **TV**: AUD. **WF**: XPF (CFP franc, pegged 119.33/EUR).
- **HT**: HTG (gourde) — confirmed, bills in gourdes.

## Zone candidates

- **MX — yes, the strongest zone case in this group.** CFE domestic tariffs 1 / 1A–1F are assigned per locality by summer temperature, and in summer the gap at 250 kWh/month (incl. IVA) is ~3x: Tarifa 1 **2.88** (CDMX, Guadalajara, Toluca) vs 1B 1.61 (Cd. Juárez), 1C **1.26** (Monterrey, Culiacán, Mazatlán, Tampico), 1D 1.24 (Reynosa, Matamoros), 1E/1F **0.99** (Mexicali, Hermosillo; at a realistic hot-summer 1,000 kWh a 1F bill is ~1.15 MXN/kWh, less with border 8% IVA). Residents absolutely know their tariff letter and state (Baja California/Sonora/Sinaloa/Tamaulipas/NL hot zones vs the altiplano). Caveat: the subsidy is **seasonal** — out of season (Nov–Apr) 1A–1F revert to ~2.1–2.8 at the same use, so the 2x+ gap holds in summer, narrows in winter. Mapping is locality-level (SEMARNAT temperature verification), not cleanly state-level: 1D/1E example-city lists differ between sources.
- **YE — noted, not a tariff zone.** Aden-grid 19 YER vs Sana'a-generator 257 YER is a ~40x real-price split residents certainly recognise, but it is a grid-vs-generator and currency split, not a billing-zone structure; handled in the Currency note instead.

## Gaps

None of the nine is left unpriced, but three rows are constructs to use with care:
- **VE**: no official tariff exists to cite; the figure is billed-amount evidence ÷ assumed consumption, and it moves monthly with devaluation.
- **YE**: the priced figure is the official grid tariff, which is nearly symbolic (hours of supply, low collection); the lived price is generator power at ~40x in real terms.
- **NU**: the tariff structure is from 2015 with no later confirmation; NPC publishes nothing.
Secondary gaps flagged inline: Haiti's TCA treatment and subscription fee; Palau's PGST status and a possible May 2026 DER rise; Wallis's abonnement amount and post-Feb-2025 TRV moves; Laos's current meter fee; Mexico's exact 1C/1D summer básico (one press snippet says 0.992 vs 1.019 here).
