# europe-small — AD AX BY FO GG GI IM IS JE MC SM VA

Researched 2026-10-04. Default consumption 300 kWh/month per the method; Iceland uses its
official 4,500 kWh/yr reference (375/month). Monthly standing charges use 365/12 = 30.42 days.

## IS — Iceland (matters most): 26.0 ISK/kWh, high

Two independent routes agree:

1. **Components (current price lists).** Energy: Orkusalan default plan 12.97 kr/kWh incl.
   24% VAT (verðskrá from 2026-05-01, orkusalan.is/verdskra; cheapest plans ~9.8). Distribution:
   Veitur (Reykjavík area, largest distributor) 11.52 kr/kWh incl. VAT from 2026-01-01
   (veitur.is news), reduced ~3.2%/kWh on 2026-04-01 when Landsnet transmission fell
   2.80→2.50 kr/kWh. Variable total ≈ 24.5 kr/kWh + fixed charges (fastagjald, both sides,
   a few hundred kr/month) ≈ **26 kr/kWh**.
2. **Official comparison.** Byggðastofnun's annual *Samanburður á orkukostnaði heimila 2025*
   (with Umhverfis- og orkustofnun, gjaldskrár as of 2025-09-01, all taxes and fixed charges in,
   reference general use 4,500 kWh/yr): Veitur þéttbýli ≈ 117 þús. kr/yr → 26.0 kr/kWh;
   HS Veitur area ≈ 115 þús.; rural RARIK/Orkubú Vestfjarða 154–173 þús. (34.2–38.4 kr/kWh);
   Grímsey (diesel) 186 þús.

Heating is overwhelmingly geothermal (and electric heating is state-subsidised per-kWh), so
general use excludes heat. GlobalPetrolPrices 26.14 ISK (Dec 2025) sanity-checks the figure.

## FO — Faroe Islands: 2.56 DKK/kWh, high

SEV (sole utility) price list from 2026-01-01: 0–10,000 kWh/yr band 2.325 DKK/kWh incl. 25%
VAT, fixed 850 DKK/yr incl. VAT. 300×2.325 + 850/12 = 697.50 + 70.83 = 768.33 → **2.561 DKK/kWh**.

## GG — Guernsey: 0.36 GBP/kWh, high

Guernsey Electricity Standard tariff from 2026-07-01 (electricity.gg news; tariff sheet mirrored
at gov.gg id=200945): 26.01 p/unit + £89.35/quarter standing (primary meter). Monthly:
78.03 + 29.78 = £107.81 → **35.9 p/kWh**. No VAT/GST in Guernsey. The standing charge is a
third of the bill at 300 kWh — heavy-use households see a lower effective rate.

## JE — Jersey: 0.25 GBP/kWh, high

Jersey Electricity General Domestic from 2026-03-01: 22.20 p/kWh + 24.21 p/day (single-phase),
rates inclusive of 5% GST. 66.60 + 7.37 = £73.97 → **24.7 p/kWh**.

## IM — Isle of Man: 0.32 GBP/kWh, high

Manx Utilities domestic from 2026-04-01: 29.5 p/kWh + 25.5 p/day (manxutilities.im rejects
automated fetches; figures via Manx Radio reporting the utility's announcement, and they are
exactly +1.5% on the April-2025 rates published on the IoM government Smart Island portal:
29.1p + 25.1p/day). Domestic electricity is VAT-zero-rated on the island. 88.50 + 7.76 =
£96.26 → **32.1 p/kWh**.

## GI — Gibraltar: 0.21 GBP/kWh, medium

Gibraltar Electricity Authority Tariff No. 3 (Domestic), as from August 2026: 11.9 p/unit +
5.8 p flexible cost adjustment = 17.7 p/unit, no consumption blocks, standing charge £5.80
(periodicity not stated on the page — assumed **monthly**) + £1.00/week meter levy. No VAT in
Gibraltar. (53.10 + 5.80 + 4.33)/300 = **21.1 p/kWh**; if the standing charge is quarterly
it is 19.8 p. Medium confidence solely for that ambiguity.

## AD — Andorra: 0.172 EUR/kWh, medium

FEDA Tarifa Plana per Decree 482/2025 (2026 tariffs = +2.8% CPI): 300 kWh/month = 10 kWh/day
falls in the ≤20 kWh/day tier at 0.1266 €/kWh; power term 2.26 €/kW/month. Contracted power
for a typical home is not published — assumed 5 kW. FEDA's billing description lists IGI as a
separate invoice line, so the general 4.5% IGI is added: (37.98 + 11.30) × 1.045 = 51.50 →
**0.1717 €/kWh**. Sensitivity: 3.3 kW → 0.164; 6.6 kW → 0.185.

## MC — Monaco: 0.249 EUR/kWh, medium (dated)

SMEG "tarif go" grid of 2024-01-01 (newest publicly posted; the current site quotes pricing
only at subscription): base option 20.99 c€/kWh TTC (TVA 20% on energy) + 11.66 €/month
subscription at 6 kVA (TVA 5.5%). (62.97 + 11.66)/300 = **24.9 c€/kWh**. Level may have moved
since January 2024; French TRV (19.4 c TTC, Feb 2026) sanity-checks the magnitude.

## SM — San Marino: 0.223 EUR/kWh, medium

AASS domestic ≤4.5 kW, tariffs from 2026-04-01 (Autorità per l'Energia delibera): energy =
PUN + 0.016180 €/kWh for the first 200 kWh/month, PUN + 0.056629 beyond; power term
0.880033 €/kW/month (×4.5 kW = 3.96). AASS's own billed-PUN table gives Aug 2026 = 0.1800
€/kWh (a spike; 2026 had mostly 0.11–0.13). 200×0.19618 + 100×0.236629 + 3.96 = 66.86 →
**0.2229 €/kWh**. At a 2026-average PUN (~0.125) it would be ~0.17. San Marino has no VAT;
no further on-bill levies found, unverified — hence medium.

## BY — Belarus: 0.3037 BYN/kWh, high

Council of Ministers Resolution No. 93 of 2026-02-25, effective 2026-03-01 (annual reset):
flat subsidised tariff for homes without electric stoves **0.3037 BYN/kWh**, electric-stove
homes 0.2581, no fixed charge; billed prices are final. Confirmed across the decree PDF
(government.by, refuses foreign IPs but is the primary URL) and several state outlets.

## AX — Åland: 0.265 EUR/kWh, high

Ålands Elandelslag (dominant distributor/supplier) Tariffhäfte 1.11.2026: variable incl.
25.5% VAT = energy 9.29 + transmission 6.07 + elskatt 2.92 = 18.28 c/kWh; fixed = 260 €/yr
(3×16A fuse) + 36.42 €/yr energy base = 296.42 €/yr. (54.84 + 24.70)/300 = **26.5 c/kWh**.
The 1 Nov edition bakes in a published 49.5% energy-charge rise (announced; until 31 Oct the
energy term is ~6.2 c incl. VAT → total ~23.4 c). Mariehamns Elnät customers differ slightly.

## Currency

- **GG, JE, IM, GI**: local pound issues (Guernsey/Jersey/Manx/Gibraltar pound) at par with
  sterling; bills are in pounds and the practical ISO code is **GBP** (GIP exists for Gibraltar
  but is 1:1). Source: each utility bills in £; e.g. jec.co.uk, electricity.gg tariff pages.
- **FO**: Faroese króna is a 1:1 version of the Danish krone — **DKK** (source: Danmarks
  Nationalbank / SEV price list in kr.).
- **AD, MC, SM, AX (Finland), VA**: **EUR** (Andorra, Monaco, San Marino use the euro under
  monetary agreements with the EU).
- **BY**: **BYN**. **IS**: **ISK**.

## Zone candidates

None reach ~2× between well-populated regions:

- **Iceland** is the closest: rural RARIK/Orkubú Vestfjarða distribution areas cost ~154–173
  þús. kr/yr vs ~115–117 in Reykjavík/HS Veitur towns (Byggðastofnun 2025) — a 1.3–1.5×
  spread, below the bar, and the expensive areas are thinly populated.
- Belarus's 0.2581 vs 0.3037 split is by stove type, not region.

## Gaps

- **VA — Vatican City**: no published household tariff and effectively no household market.
  Electricity is supplied from Italy (ACEA) under the Lateran-treaty arrangements and paid by
  the Governorate; the ~800 residents live in service housing and are not billed per-kWh on a
  public tariff. Priced-out option if one is ever needed: Italian ARERA maggior-tutela rate.
