# west-c-mid-a: LR, SL, CV, SH, TD, CF, ST, GQ

Note: I sourced SH, CV and ST from the utility or regulator pages through direct fetches and Wayback captures, and LR and SL from regulator documents plus press. TD, CF and GQ could not be properly researched.

## LR Liberia
- Source: LERC tariff order released 2025-12-12, in force 2026-01-01 to 2028-12-31, as reported by FrontPageAfrica (https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-lerc-announces-electricity-tariff-reductions-new-rates-take-effect-january-2026/) and allAfrica (https://allafrica.com/stories/202512160350.html). LEC tariff page: https://lecliberia.com/customer-service/payment-billing/current-tariff/ (partly updated: the social row shows 0.130 + GST 13%).
- Structure: social 0.13 USD/kWh (<=25 kWh, no fixed charge). Residential prepaid 0.22/kWh + 2.00/month. Residential postpaid 0.22/kWh + 3.79/month. Billed in USD.
- GST: the LEC page adds GST to the energy charge and the fixed charge (residential totals of 0.264 and 2.728 in the 2022 schedule). GST rose from 12% to 13% on 2026-01-01 (https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/11/13/liberia-to-raise-gst-to-13-in-2026-plans-18-vat-launch-in-2027/). VAT at 18% is planned for 2027.
- Bill at 200 kWh, prepaid: 200 x 0.22 = 44.00, plus fixed 2.00, gives 46.00. GST 13% adds 5.98, for a total of 51.98. 51.98 / 200 = **0.26 USD/kWh**.
- Caveats: the 2026 press reports quote rates before GST. Whether GST is 13% or 10% on the fixed part changes the result by less than 0.001.

## SL Sierra Leone
- Source: the SLEWRC "Approved tariff ... for EDSA effective 1st October 2023", uploaded to ewrc.gov.sl in 2025-02 (https://ewrc.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Approval-for-Gazette-1.pdf, read via its Wayback copy). Rates are quoted in old leones (SLL); 1 SLE = 1000 SLL.
- Structure (T1 residential, prepaid and postpaid, including GST 15%):
  - 0-25 kWh: 2.092 SLE
  - 25-200 kWh: 4.853 SLE
  - over 200 kWh: 5.271 SLE
  - Service charge: 11.000 SLE/month prepaid, 13.000 postpaid, no GST.
- Bill at 200 kWh, prepaid:
  - 25 x 2.092 = 52.300
  - 175 x 4.853 = 849.275
  - Service charge 11.000
  - Total 912.575; 912.575 / 200 = **4.563 SLE/kWh**.
- Caveats: I found no newer approved tariff, but 2025-26 news was not fully checked. EDSA's prepaid meters dominate.

## CV Cabo Verde
- Source: ARME, "ARME atualiza Tarifas de Eletricidade para EDEC e AEB em 2026" (Deliberação 74/CA/2025, in force 2026-01-01; Wayback capture of arme.cv, article id 1306). The 2025 ARME tariff sheet confirms the structure: two domestic bands, IVA 8% (reduced rate, OE 2024 art. 75), and no fixed charge in the table.
- EDEC low-voltage domestic, including IVA: up to 60 kWh 24.55 CVE, above 60 kWh 32.20 CVE. Tarifa social: 14.73 / 17.18 / 25.76 for the 0-30 / 30-60 / 60-90 kWh bands. Boa Vista (AEB): 29.92 / 37.57.
- Bill at 300 kWh (default for non-low-income countries): 60 x 24.55 = 1,473.00, plus 240 x 32.20 = 7,728.00, total 9,201.00. 9,201 / 300 = **30.67 CVE/kWh**.
- Caveats: I treated the bands as progressive; if a household above 60 kWh pays 32.20 on every kWh, the figure is 32.20. ARME lists a "BO 01/09/2026 - Eletricidade" document that I could not download (arme.cv refused connections, no archive copy), so a mid-2026 change cannot be ruled out. I found no audiovisual or other levy.

## SH Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha
- Source: Connect Saint Helena press release of 2026-08-19, "Increase in electricity tariff - effective 1 September 2026" (http://www.connect.co.sh/uploads/2/9/0/2/29020443/260819_changes_to_electricity_tariffs_effective_1_september_2026.pdf). The fuel-price mechanism started 2025-07-01 under URA directive EX-GAZ-62 (http://www.connect.co.sh/uploads/2/9/0/2/29020443/250612_changes_to_water_and_electricity_tariffs_effective_1_july_2025.pdf).
- Build-up per kWh:

  | Component | £/kWh |
  |---|---|
  | Fuel | 0.37 |
  | Overheads | 0.24 |
  | Initial subsidy | -0.07 |
  | Additional SHG subsidy | -0.02 |
  | Domestic discount | -0.05 |
  | Domestic total, up to 1,000 kWh | **0.47** |
  | Commercial / domestic above 1,000 kWh | 0.58 |

- There is no standing charge for occupied properties; the £45/quarter charge applies only to empty properties. St Helena has no VAT. Bills are now monthly.
- Bill at 300 kWh: 300 x 0.47 = 141.00, giving **0.47 SHP/kWh**.
- Ascension Island and Tristan da Cunha have separate small island-government supplies. I found no published household tariff, so St Helena island stands for the territory.

## TD Chad
- No SNE household tariff schedule found. The World Bank Chad Energy Access Scale Up PAD (2022-03; https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/860701648216750651/text/IBArchive-bd2c789e-ee04-4df7-a219-9409a5f705d3.txt) says SNE tariffs range from 85 to 125 XAF/kWh (US$0.15-0.22). That range covers all customer categories, and the PAD does not say whether VAT is included. The regulator ARSE (https://arse.td/, texte-reglementaire page) publishes laws and decrees but no tariff grid.
- Price left empty (gap).

## CF Central African Republic
- Source: the World Bank CAR Emergency Electricity Supply and Access Project PAD (2019; http://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/307711551668592488/text/SIERRA-LEONE-PAD-02192019-636872473755315841.txt; the file name is mislabelled "SIERRA-LEONE" but the content is CAR). Its financial model gives an ENERCA average tariff of 83 XAF/kWh (US$0.14, 2017). The 2022 ESSAP PAD (https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/824551653593686389/pdf/Central-African-Republic-First-Phase-of-the-Electricity-Sector-Strengthening-and-Access-Project.pdf) repeats "average tariff US$0.14/kWh" and notes many flat-rate customers billed by hours of use.
- Result: **83 XAF/kWh**, low confidence. This is an average across all customers, not a household tariff, and the tax treatment is unknown.

## ST Sao Tome and Principe
- Source: the EMAE tariff page (https://www.emae.st/PT/clientes/tarifarios), live in 2026-09. The figures are identical in Wayback captures from 2021-11, 2023-08 and 2024-11.
- Domestic postpaid: up to 100 kWh 1.67, up to 300 kWh 2.45, above 300 kWh 3.84 STN/kWh. Domestic prepaid: 2.75 STN/kWh single-phase, 3.35 three-phase. Meter rental: 6 STN/month (10/30 A) or 17 STN/month (20/60 A) single-phase.
- Bill at 300 kWh, postpaid, progressive bands: 100 x 1.67 = 167.00, plus 200 x 2.45 = 490.00, plus meter rental 6.00, total 663.00. 663 / 300 = **2.21 STN/kWh**. If the band applies to all kWh the figure is 2.47; prepaid is 2.75.
- Caveats: the page gives no validity date. IVA (introduced 2023) is not shown, and I did not verify whether it applies to household electricity. I could not establish whether prepaid or postpaid is the more common contract.

## GQ Equatorial Guinea
- No SEGESA household tariff found. segesa.gq does not resolve. The World Bank GQ Economic Updates 2023-2025 and the 2025 Country Economic Memorandum mention SEGESA but give no tariff. Gap.

## Currency notes
- LR: LEC tariffs are set and billed in **USD** (LERC tariff order; LEC tariff page). LRD is also legal tender.
- SL: **SLE** (new leone, redenominated 2022 at 1 SLE = 1000 SLL). The SLEWRC tariff document still quotes SLL, so I divided by 1000.
- ST: **STN** (new dobra, redenominated 2018 at 1 STN = 1000 STD). EMAE quotes "dobras" at STN magnitudes.
- SH: **SHP**, at par with GBP; Connect quotes in £.
- CV: CVE (pegged to EUR). TD, CF, GQ: XAF.

## Zone candidates
none. Cabo Verde's Boa Vista (AEB) is about 20% above EDEC, well short of 2x. St Helena, Ascension and Tristan run separate systems, but Ascension and Tristan are tiny and have no published tariff.
