# africa-north-west / north: DZ EG EH LY MA SD TN

## DZ Algeria

- Source: CREG decision D/22-15/CD of 2015-12-29. HT tariffs have applied since 2016-01-01. The leaflet https://creg.gov.dz/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Tarif-BT-BP-FR.pdf has the quarterly thresholds. The current tariff page https://creg.gov.dz/consommateurs/tarification/ (tables re-published 2025-11) shows the same 54M rates. Decision PDF: https://creg.gov.dz/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/D_22-15_CD_FR.pdf (scanned; OCR read).
- Tariff 54M (households) is progressive per quarter: T1 0-125 kWh 1.7787 DA; T2 126-250 kWh 4.1789 DA; T3 251-1000 kWh 4.8120 DA; T4 >1000 kWh 5.4796 DA. The power charge is 4.37 DA/kW/month on the PMD.
- Typical use: CREG Chiffres clés 2024 gives 45,798 GWh BT sales over 12.22 M BT customers, about 312 kWh/month. Computed at 300 kWh/month, which is 900 kWh/quarter.
- Quarter, energy: 125 x 1.7787 = 222.34, plus 125 x 4.1789 = 522.36, plus 650 x 4.8120 = 3,127.80. Energy total is 3,872.50 DA.
- Quarter, power charge for 6 kW single-phase: 78.66 DA (annex: 78.7). HT total is 3,951.16 DA.
- TVA at 9% on everything: 355.60. Taxe d'habitation: 75 DA/quarter, which Sonelgaz's FAQ says applies in daïra seats and all of Algiers, Oran, Constantine and Annaba. Total 4,381.76 DA / 900 = **4.87 DZD/kWh**. Without the taxe d'habitation it is 4.79.
- Caveats:
  - The household TVA rate is not confirmed from an official page. CREG's consumer guide example shows an older 7%; the reduced rate has been 9% since 2017. If 19% applies to consumption above 250 kWh/quarter, the figure is 5.22.
  - Excluded: the "droit fixe (RTA)" radio-TV fee, stamp duty on cash payment, and the CREG cost contribution of 0.0075 DA/kWh (negligible).
  - Not verified: possible summer discounts for southern wilayas.

## EG Egypt

- Source: EgyptERA, tariff in force from April 2026 consumption, https://egyptera.org/en/TarrifApril2026.aspx (the Arabic index page links it as "التعريفة السارية بدءاً من استهلاك ابريل 2026").
- Residential tariff, in piasters/kWh:
  - Consumers up to 100 kWh: 0-50 at 68, 51-100 at 78.
  - Consumers of 101-1000 kWh: 0-200 at 95, 201-350 at 155, 351-650 at 195.
  - Consumers above 650 kWh: 210 flat (0-1000).
  - Consumers above 1000 kWh: 258 flat. This is the only residential tier changed versus the Aug 2024 tariff.
  - Monthly customer-service fee by band: 1 / 2 / 6 / 11 / 15 / 25 / 40 EGP.
- Bill at 300 kWh (the method's default; no sourced typical use): 200 x 0.95 = 190.00, plus 100 x 1.55 = 155.00, plus the 11.00 fee, gives 356.00 EGP. Divided by 300 that is **1.19 EGP/kWh**.
- Other references: at 200 kWh the bill is 196 EGP, which is 0.98. ERA lists no VAT. Residential customers are exempt from the consumption stamp; the only stamp is an annual 3 EGP import stamp, which is negligible. Prepaid meters use the same tariff.
- Caveat: the municipal cleaning fee that some governorates collect on electricity bills is excluded, because it varies by area.

## EH Western Sahara

- Status: a UN-listed non-self-governing territory whose status is disputed. Most of the population lives in the part administered by Morocco (Laayoune, Dakhla, Smara, Boujdour), where ONEE supplies electricity at the national Moroccan tariff, billed in MAD. The area east of the berm is very sparsely populated and has no utility billing data.
- Arithmetic is the same as MA: 100 kWh x 0.9010 MAD TTC gives **0.90 MAD/kWh**.
- Caveat (low confidence): Morocco's southern provinces have long-standing tax exemptions. It is not verified whether VAT is actually charged on local electricity bills. Without VAT (if the HT price were simply billed) the figure would be about 0.76.

## LY Libya

- Source: World Bank / Strategy& (PwC), "GECOL ... tariff framework review", July 2017, p.16. It gives the residential tariff as 20 Dhs/kWh (0.020 LYD) over 2011-2017; residential is the only class with mild volume dependence. https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/259641527050487229/pdf/Task-C-GECOL-Institutional-dev-and-perform-improvement-tariff-framework-review-tariff-structure-set-up-and-reform-pathway.pdf
- Result: **0.020 LYD/kWh** for energy only. No fixed charge or tax could be confirmed.
- Caveats:
  - GECOL's website (gecol.ly) refused connections, so the figure is not confirmed as current.
  - Libya Observer (2025-05-05) reports a GECOL increase for factories and businesses at summer peak only, to 1,000 dirhams/kW. No residential change was found.
  - Collection is weak, so effective household payments are often lower.
  - Low confidence.

## MA Morocco

- Source: ONEE Branche Électricité, residential tariffs page: https://www.one.org.ma/FR/pages/interne.asp?esp=1&id1=3&id2=113&t2=1 (expired TLS certificate; fetched 2026-10-04).
- Domestic tariff in DH/kWh TTC: 0-100 at 0.9010; 101-150 at 1.0732; 151-200 at 1.0732; 201-300 at 1.1676; 301-500 at 1.3817; above 500 at 1.5958.
- How the blocks apply: up to 150 kWh the blocks are progressive. Above 150 kWh billing is selective: the whole month's consumption is charged at the rate of the band it falls in, with a 10 kWh tolerance.
- Typical use: the budget minister said two-thirds of consumers sit in the lowest block (le360, 2024-01-15, https://fr.le360.ma/societe/hausse-de-la-tva-sur-les-factures-deau-et-delectricite-ce-qui-va-changer-dans-vos-montants-mensuels_BKIJGJTLDVBMVFWFDLURFXLB74/). Computed at 100 kWh: 100 x 0.9010 = 90.10 DH, which is **0.90 MAD/kWh**.
- At other consumption levels:
  - 200 kWh: all at 1.0732, giving 1.07.
  - 300 kWh: all at 1.1676, giving 1.17, plus TPPAN.
- TPPAN (audiovisual tax): charged on bills only above 200 kWh/month (le360, 2019, https://fr.le360.ma/societe/taxe-pour-la-promotion-du-paysage-audiovisuel-les-precisions-del-othmani-205173/), so it is not due at 100 kWh.
- VAT: the reform schedule was 14% → 16% (2024) → 18% (2025) → 20% (2026-01). ONEE's page still says "TVA 18%" and the TTC prices are the same long-standing ones; Lydec said the change "n'aura aucun impact sur les tarifs". So the TTC grid is used as published.
- Caveats: meter rental/maintenance is excluded (amount not verified). The same national grid applies at the regional distributors (Lydec, Redal, Amendis, the régies).

## SD Sudan

- Gap. No current SEDC residential (prepaid) tariff could be confirmed:
  - SEDC and Ministry sites did not resolve.
  - The only World Bank tariff diagnostic found is from 2019. It predates the 2021-2022 increases and the currency collapse, so it is unusable.
  - Radio Dabanga's 2023-2026 coverage found no tariff figures, only outages and grid damage.
- Since the April 2023 war, much of Khartoum, Darfur and Kordofan has no grid supply. Where SEDC still bills (Port Sudan, the north and east), it is prepaid in SDG.

## TN Tunisia

- Source: STEG, "Les Tarifs d'électricité", BT tariff HT from 2022-05-01: https://www.steg.com.tn/fr/page/les-tarifs-d%C3%A9lectricit%C3%A9
- Residential tariff (mill/kWh):
  - Economy band (1-2 kVA, ≤100 kWh/month): 62 if ≤50 kWh, 96 if 51-100 kWh.
  - 101-200 kWh: 176.
  - 201-300 kWh: 218.
  - 301-500 kWh: 341.
  - Above 500 kWh: 414.
  - The footnotes ("si la consommation est comprise entre …") price the whole month at the band reached.
- Charges and taxes:
  - Power charge: 700 mill/kVA/month.
  - TVA: 7% on residential energy if consumption is ≤300 kWh/month, otherwise 13%. The footnote also says 19% "sur toutes les redevances".
  - Municipal surtax: 5 mill/kWh.
  - FTE: 5 mill/kWh, except economy-band customers at ≤100 kWh.
- Typical use: STEG says 85% of customers use ≤200 kWh/month (Kapitalis, 2022-05-16, https://kapitalis.com/tunisie/2022/05/16/tunisie-la-steg-explique-la-nouvelle-hausse-des-prix-du-gaz-et-de-lelectricite/). Computed at 200 kWh with 2 kVA.
- Bill at 200 kWh:
  - Energy: 200 x 0.176 = 35.200 DT.
  - TVA 7%: 2.464.
  - Surtax: 1.000.
  - FTE: 1.000.
  - Power charge: 2 x 0.700 = 1.400, plus 19% TVA of 0.266.
  - Total 41.330 DT / 200 = **0.207 TND/kWh**.
- Caveats:
  - Non-official sites list a radio-TV fee (about 1.8 DT) on STEG bills. It is not on STEG's tariff page, so it is not verified and is excluded; including it would add about 0.009.
  - Bills are bimonthly.
  - No tariff change has been reported since 2022.

## Currency notes

- DZ: DZD. EG: EGP; tariffs are quoted in piasters, and ERA states the tariff is indexed to the CBE official exchange rate. MA and EH: MAD (EH is billed in MAD by ONEE). TN: TND; tariffs are quoted in millimes, 1 TND = 1,000 mill. LY: LYD; tariffs are quoted in dirhams, 1 LYD = 1,000 Dhs.
- LY has a gap between the official and parallel exchange rates, but bills are in LYD.
- SD: SDG, with a large gap between the official and parallel rates since the war; billing is prepaid in SDG.
